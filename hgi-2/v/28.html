Sxemani tahlil qilish va tok yo’nalishlarini tanlash

Sxemada 4 ta tugun (a, b, c, d) va 6 ta tarmoq bor (n = 4, m = 6). Har bir tarmoq raqami undagi rezistor raqamiga teng:

Tarmoq Tarkibi Ulanish tugunlari Tokning tanlangan yo’nalishi
1 R₁, E₁ a – b a → b
2 R₂, E₂ a – c c → a
3 R₃, E₃ b – d b → d
4 R₄ a – d a → d
5 R₅ b – c b → c
6 R₆ c – d d → c

Kirxgofning I qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: n − 1 = 4 − 1 = 3. Kirxgofning II qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: m − (n − 1) = 6 − 3 = 3. Jami 6 ta tenglama — 6 ta noma’lum tokni topish uchun yetarli.

Tarmoq toklarining shartli musbat yo’nalishlari 2-rasmda qizil strelkalar bilan ko’rsatilgan. Konturlarni aylanib chiqish yo’nalishi — soat mili bo’yicha (yashil strelkalar).

1. Kirxgof qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish

Kirxgofning birinchi qonuni (tugunga kiruvchi toklar «+», chiquvchi toklar «−» ishora bilan olinadi):

5.2. Ekvivalent qarshilik RekvR_{ekv} ni aniqlash

Barcha EYuK manbalarini ularning ichki qarshiligi (nol) bilan almashtiramiz va 4-tarmoqni uzilgan holda qoldiramiz (6-rasm). Hosil bo’lgan passiv sxemani a va d qisqichlarga nisbatan bosqichma-bosqich soddalashtiramiz.

a, b, c tugunlar orasidagi R1R_1, R5R_5, R2R_2 qarshiliklar uchburchak hosil qiladi. Uni ekvivalent yulduzga almashtiramiz (yulduz markazi — O nuqta):

Ra=R1⋅R2R1+R5+R2=10⋅721=3,3333Om;Rb=R1⋅R5R1+R5+R2=10⋅421=1,9048Om;Rc=R2⋅R5R1+R5+R2=7⋅421=1,3333Om. \begin{aligned} R_{a} &= \dfrac{R_1\cdot R_2}{R_1 + R_5 + R_2} = \dfrac{10\cdot 7}{21} = 3{,}3333\ \text{Om};\\ R_{b} &= \dfrac{R_1\cdot R_5}{R_1 + R_5 + R_2} = \dfrac{10\cdot 4}{21} = 1{,}9048\ \text{Om};\\ R_{c} &= \dfrac{R_2\cdot R_5}{R_1 + R_5 + R_2} = \dfrac{7\cdot 4}{21} = 1{,}3333\ \text{Om}. \end{aligned}

b nuqta orqali R3R_3 va RbR_{b} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re1=R3+Rb=9+1,9048=10,9048Om.R_{e1} = R_3 + R_{b} = 9 + 1{,}9048 = 10{,}9048\ \text{Om}.

c nuqta orqali R6R_6 va RcR_{c} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re2=R6+Rc=8+1,3333=9,3333Om.R_{e2} = R_6 + R_{c} = 8 + 1{,}3333 = 9{,}3333\ \text{Om}.

d va O nuqtalar orasidagi Re1R_{e1} va Re2R_{e2} qarshiliklar parallel ulangan:

Re3=Re1⋅Re2Re1+Re2=10,9048⋅9,333310,9048+9,3333=5,0290Om.R_{e3} = \dfrac{R_{e1}\cdot R_{e2}}{R_{e1} + R_{e2}} = \dfrac{10{,}9048\cdot 9{,}3333}{10{,}9048 + 9{,}3333} = 5{,}0290\ \text{Om}.

O nuqta orqali RaR_{a} va Re3R_{e3} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re4=Ra+Re3=3,3333+5,0290=8,3624Om.R_{e4} = R_{a} + R_{e3} = 3{,}3333 + 5{,}0290 = 8{,}3624\ \text{Om}.

Natijada: Rekv=8,3624R_{ekv} = 8{,}3624 Om.

5.3. I₄ tokini hisoblash

I4=UxxR4+Rekv=64,70612+8,3624=64,70620,3624=3,178A. I_4 = \dfrac{U_{xx}}{R_4 + R_{ekv}} = \dfrac{64{,}706}{12 + 8{,}3624} = \dfrac{64{,}706}{20{,}3624} = 3{,}178\ \text{A}.

Kontur toklar usulida topilgan qiymat bilan solishtiramiz:

γ=|I4−I4,eg|I4⋅100%=|3,178−3,178|3,178⋅100%=0,00%. \gamma = \dfrac{|I_4 - I_{4,eg}|}{I_4}\cdot 100\% = \dfrac{|3{,}178 - 3{,}178|}{3{,}178}\cdot 100\% = 0{,}00\%.

Ekvivalent generator usuli natijasi kontur toklar va tugun potensiallar usullari natijalari bilan mos keldi.

6. Quvvat muvozanati

Manbalar quvvati: EYuK yo’nalishi tok yo’nalishiga mos kelsa, manba energiya beradi (E·I «+» ishora bilan), teskari bo’lsa — energiya iste’mol qiladi («−» ishora bilan). 1-tarmoqda I₁ toki E₁ ga teskari yo’nalgan, shuning uchun E₁ manbai iste’molchi rejimida ishlaydi.

Pmanba=−E1I1+E2I2+E3I3=−60⋅1,015+100⋅4,192+40⋅0,887=−60,90+419,20+35,48=393,78Vt. P_{manba} = -E_1I_1 + E_2I_2 + E_3I_3 = -60\cdot 1{,}015 + 100\cdot 4{,}192 + 40\cdot 0{,}887 = -60{,}90 + 419{,}20 + 35{,}48 = 393{,}78\ \text{Vt}.

Iste’molchilar (rezistorlar) quvvati:

Pist=I12R1+I22R2+I32R3+I42R4+I52R5+I62R6==1,0152⋅10+4,1922⋅7+0,8872⋅9+3,1782⋅12+0,1272⋅4+4,0652⋅8==10,302+123,010+7,081+121,196+0,065+132,194=393,848Vt. \begin{aligned} P_{ist} &= I_1^2R_1 + I_2^2R_2 + I_3^2R_3 + I_4^2R_4 + I_5^2R_5 + I_6^2R_6 =\\ &= 1{,}015^2\cdot 10 + 4{,}192^2\cdot 7 + 0{,}887^2\cdot 9 + 3{,}178^2\cdot 12 + 0{,}127^2\cdot 4 + 4{,}065^2\cdot 8 =\\ &= 10{,}302 + 123{,}010 + 7{,}081 + 121{,}196 + 0{,}065 + 132{,}194 = 393{,}848\ \text{Vt}. \end{aligned}

Hisoblash xatoligi:

γ=|Pmanba−Pist|Pmanba⋅100%=|393,78−393,848|393,78⋅100%=0,0172%. \gamma = \dfrac{|P_{manba} - P_{ist}|}{P_{manba}}\cdot 100\% = \dfrac{|393{,}78 - 393{,}848|}{393{,}78}\cdot 100\% = 0{,}0172\%.

Quvvat muvozanati bajarildi: Pmanba≈PistP_{manba} \approx P_{ist} (farq faqat toklarni yaxlitlash bilan bog’liq).
