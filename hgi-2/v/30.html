Topshiriq. Berilgan sxema uchun:

  1. Kirxgofning birinchi va ikkinchi qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish;
  2. kontur toklar usuli bo’yicha tarmoq toklarini topish;
  3. tugun potensiallar usuli bo’yicha tarmoq toklarini topish;
  4. topilgan toklarni Kirxgofning I va II qonunlari asosida tekshirish;

Sxemani tahlil qilish va tok yo’nalishlarini tanlash

Sxemada 4 ta tugun (a, b, c, d) va 6 ta tarmoq bor (n = 4, m = 6). Har bir tarmoq raqami undagi rezistor raqamiga teng:

Tarmoq Tarkibi Ulanish tugunlari Tokning tanlangan yo’nalishi
1 R₁, E₁ c – d c → d
2 R₂, E₂ b – d d → b
3 R₃, E₃ a – b b → a
4 R₄ a – c a → c
5 R₅ a – d a → d
6 R₆ b – c b → c

Kirxgofning I qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: n − 1 = 4 − 1 = 3. Kirxgofning II qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: m − (n − 1) = 6 − 3 = 3. Jami 6 ta tenglama — 6 ta noma’lum tokni topish uchun yetarli.

Tarmoq toklarining shartli musbat yo’nalishlari 2-rasmda qizil strelkalar bilan ko’rsatilgan. Konturlarni aylanib chiqish yo’nalishi — soat mili bo’yicha (yashil strelkalar).

1. Kirxgof qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish

Kirxgofning birinchi qonuni (tugunga kiruvchi toklar «+», chiquvchi toklar «−» ishora bilan olinadi):

«a» tugun uchun:I3−I4−I5=0;«b» tugun uchun:I2−I3−I6=0;«c» tugun uchun:−I1+I4+I6=0;«d» tugun uchun:I1−I2+I5=0.(tekshirish uchun) \begin{aligned} &\text{«a» tugun uchun:} && I_3 - I_4 - I_5 = 0;\\ &\text{«b» tugun uchun:} && I_2 - I_3 - I_6 = 0;\\ &\text{«c» tugun uchun:} && -I_1 + I_4 + I_6 = 0;\\ &\text{«d» tugun uchun:} && I_1 - I_2 + I_5 = 0. \quad (\text{tekshirish uchun}) \end{aligned}

Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni (tok yoki EYuK yo’nalishi kontur aylanish yo’nalishiga mos kelsa «+», teskari bo’lsa «−»):

I kontur (a–c–b–a):I4R4−I6R6+I3R3=E3;II kontur (d–a–b–d):−I5R5−I3R3−I2R2=−E3−E2;III kontur (d–b–c–d):I2R2+I6R6+I1R1=E2+E1. \begin{aligned} &\text{I kontur (a–c–b–a):} && I_4R_4 - I_6R_6 + I_3R_3 = E_3;\\ &\text{II kontur (d–a–b–d):} && -I_5R_5 - I_3R_3 - I_2R_2 = -E_3 - E_2;\\ &\text{III kontur (d–b–c–d):} && I_2R_2 + I_6R_6 + I_1R_1 = E_2 + E_1. \end{aligned}

Son qiymatlarini qo’ysak:

{I3−I4−I5=0I2−I3−I6=0−I1+I4+I6=07I4−12I6+10I3=50−9I5−10I3−8I2=−1508I2+12I6+4I1=180 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &I_3 - I_4 - I_5 = 0\\ &I_2 - I_3 - I_6 = 0\\ &-I_1 + I_4 + I_6 = 0\\ &7I_4 - 12I_6 + 10I_3 = 50\\ &-9I_5 - 10I_3 - 8I_2 = -150\\ &8I_2 + 12I_6 + 4I_1 = 180 \end{aligned} \right.

Bu 6 noma’lumli tenglamalar sistemasining yechimi quyida kontur toklar va tugun potensiallar usullari yordamida topiladi.

2. Kontur toklar usuli

Kontur toklarini I₁₁, I₂₂, I₃₃ bilan belgilab, ularni soat mili yo’nalishida yo’naltiramiz (3-rasm).

Kontur toklar usuli tenglamalarining umumiy ko’rinishi:

{I11(R3+R4+R6)−I22R3−I33R6=E3−I11R3+I22(R2+R3+R5)−I33R2=−E2−E3−I11R6−I22R2+I33(R1+R2+R6)=E1+E2 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &I_{11}(R_3+R_4+R_6) - I_{22}R_3 - I_{33}R_6 = E_3\\ &-I_{11}R_3 + I_{22}(R_2+R_3+R_5) - I_{33}R_2 = -E_2 - E_3\\ &-I_{11}R_6 - I_{22}R_2 + I_{33}(R_1+R_2+R_6) = E_1 + E_2 \end{aligned} \right.

Bu yerda: R₁₁ = R₃ + R₄ + R₆ = 10 + 7 + 12 = 29 Om; R₂₂ = R₂ + R₃ + R₅ = 8 + 10 + 9 = 27 Om; R₃₃ = R₁ + R₂ + R₆ = 4 + 8 + 12 = 24 Om — konturlarning xususiy qarshiliklari; R₁₂ = R₃ = 10 Om, R₁₃ = R₆ = 12 Om, R₂₃ = R₂ = 8 Om — qo’shni konturlarning umumiy qarshiliklari (kontur toklari umumiy tarmoqda qarama-qarshi yo’nalgani uchun tenglamada «−» ishora bilan olinadi). Konturlarning EYuKlari (aylanish yo’nalishiga mos EYuK «+», teskari EYuK «−»): E₁₁ = E₃ = 50 V; E₂₂ = −E₂ − E₃ = -150 V; E₃₃ = E₁ + E₂ = 180 V.

Son qiymatlarini qo’yamiz:

{29I11−10I22−12I33=50−10I11+27I22−8I33=−150−12I11−8I22+24I33=180 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &29\,I_{11} - 10\,I_{22} - 12\,I_{33} = 50\\ &-10\,I_{11} + 27\,I_{22} - 8\,I_{33} = -150\\ &-12\,I_{11} - 8\,I_{22} + 24\,I_{33} = 180 \end{aligned} \right.

Tenglamalar sistemasini Kramer usuli bilan yechamiz. Bosh determinant:

Δ=∣29−10−12−1027−8−12−824∣=8728.\Delta = \begin{vmatrix} 29 & -10 & -12 \\ -10 & 27 & -8 \\ -12 & -8 & 24 \end{vmatrix} = 8728.

Yordamchi determinantlar (tegishli ustun ozod hadlar bilan almashtiriladi):

Δ11=∣50−10−12−15027−8180−824∣=51520.\Delta_{11} = \begin{vmatrix} 50 & -10 & -12 \\ -150 & 27 & -8 \\ 180 & -8 & 24 \end{vmatrix} = 51520.

Δ22=∣2950−12−10−150−8−1218024∣=−2640.\Delta_{22} = \begin{vmatrix} 29 & 50 & -12 \\ -10 & -150 & -8 \\ -12 & 180 & 24 \end{vmatrix} = -2640.

Δ33=∣29−1050−1027−150−12−8180∣=90340.\Delta_{33} = \begin{vmatrix} 29 & -10 & 50 \\ -10 & 27 & -150 \\ -12 & -8 & 180 \end{vmatrix} = 90340.

Kontur toklari:

I11=Δ11Δ=515208728=5,903A;I22=Δ22Δ=−26408728=−0,302A;I33=Δ33Δ=903408728=10,351A. \begin{aligned} I_{11} &= \dfrac{\Delta_{11}}{\Delta} = \dfrac{51520}{8728} = 5{,}903\ \text{A};\\ I_{22} &= \dfrac{\Delta_{22}}{\Delta} = \dfrac{-2640}{8728} = -0{,}302\ \text{A};\\ I_{33} &= \dfrac{\Delta_{33}}{\Delta} = \dfrac{90340}{8728} = 10{,}351\ \text{A}. \end{aligned}

I₂₂ ning manfiy chiqishi bu kontur tokining haqiqiy yo’nalishi soat miliga teskari ekanini bildiradi.

Kontur toklari orqali tarmoq toklarini aniqlaymiz (tarmoq tokining yo’nalishi bilan mos kontur toki «+», teskari kontur toki «−» bilan olinadi):

I1=I33=10,351A;I2=−I22+I33=−(−0,302)+10,351=10,653A;I3=I11−I22=5,903−(−0,302)=6,205A;I4=I11=5,903A;I5=−I22=−(−0,302)=0,302A;I6=−I11+I33=−5,903+10,351=4,448A. \begin{aligned} I_1 &= I_{33} = 10{,}351\ \text{A};\\ I_2 &= -I_{22} + I_{33} = -(-0{,}302) + 10{,}351 = 10{,}653\ \text{A};\\ I_3 &= I_{11} - I_{22} = 5{,}903 - (-0{,}302) = 6{,}205\ \text{A};\\ I_4 &= I_{11} = 5{,}903\ \text{A};\\ I_5 &= -I_{22} = -(-0{,}302) = 0{,}302\ \text{A};\\ I_6 &= -I_{11} + I_{33} = -5{,}903 + 10{,}351 = 4{,}448\ \text{A}. \end{aligned}

Barcha tarmoq toklari musbat chiqdi, demak 2-rasmda tanlangan yo’nalishlar toklarning haqiqiy yo’nalishlari bilan mos keladi.

4. Toklarni Kirxgof qonunlari bo’yicha tekshirish

Kirxgofning birinchi qonuni bo’yicha:

5. Ekvivalent generator usuli bilan I₄ tokini aniqlash

Ekvivalent generator usuliga ko’ra 4-tarmoqqa nisbatan qolgan barcha zanjir EYuKi Eekv=UxxE_{ekv} = U_{xx} va ichki qarshiligi RekvR_{ekv} bo’lgan bitta ekvivalent manba bilan almashtiriladi (7-rasm). Shunda:

I4=UxxR4+Rekv,I_4 = \dfrac{U_{xx}}{R_4 + R_{ekv}},

bu yerda Uxx=φa−φcU_{xx} = \varphi_a - \varphi_c — 4-tarmoq uzilgandagi (salt ishlash rejimidagi) a va c tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish; RekvR_{ekv} — EYuKlar qisqa tutashtirilgandagi zanjirning a va c qisqichlariga nisbatan kirish qarshiligi.

5.1. Salt ishlash kuchlanishi UxxU_{xx} ni aniqlash

4-tarmoq uzilganda (5-rasm) a va c nuqtalar tugun bo’lmay qoladi va ular orqali o’tgan tarmoqlar ketma-ket ulanadi. Natijada zanjir b va d tugunlar orasida parallel ulangan uchta tarmoqdan iborat bo’ladi:

  • b–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₂ = 8 Om, b tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK E₂ = 100 V;
  • b–a–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₃ + R₅ = 10 + 9 = 19 Om, b tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK −E₃ = -50 V;
  • b–c–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₆ + R₁ = 12 + 4 = 16 Om, b tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK −E₁ = -80 V;

Ikki tugun usuli bo’yicha b va d tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish (φd=0\varphi_d = 0):

Ubd=φb=E2R2+−E3R3+R5+−E1R6+R11R2+1R3+R5+1R6+R1=1008−5019−801618+119+116=4,86840,2401=20,274V. U_{bd} = \varphi_b = \dfrac{\dfrac{E_2}{R_2} + \dfrac{-E_3}{R_3+R_5} + \dfrac{-E_1}{R_6+R_1}}{\dfrac{1}{R_2}+\dfrac{1}{R_3+R_5}+\dfrac{1}{R_6+R_1}} = \dfrac{\dfrac{100}{8} - \dfrac{50}{19} - \dfrac{80}{16}}{\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac{1}{19}+\dfrac{1}{16}} = \dfrac{4{,}8684}{0{,}2401} = 20{,}274\ \text{V}.

Salt ishlash rejimidagi tarmoq toklari (b tugundan d tugunga qarab hisoblanadi; manfiy qiymat tok aslida teskari yo’nalganini bildiradi):

I2xx=φb−φd−E2R2=20,274−0−1008=−9,966A(yo’l b–d;tok d→b);I3xx=I5xx=φb−φd+E3R3+R5=20,274−0+5019=3,699A(yo’l b–a–d;tok b→d);I6xx=I1xx=φb−φd+E1R6+R1=20,274−0+8016=6,267A(yo’l b–c–d;tok b→d). \begin{aligned} I_{2xx} &= \dfrac{\varphi_b - \varphi_d - E_2}{R_2} = \dfrac{20{,}274 - 0 - 100}{8} = -9{,}966\ \text{A} \quad (\text{yo'l } b–d;\ \text{tok } d\to b);\\ I_{3xx} = I_{5xx} &= \dfrac{\varphi_b - \varphi_d + E_3}{R_3+R_5} = \dfrac{20{,}274 - 0 + 50}{19} = 3{,}699\ \text{A} \quad (\text{yo'l } b–a–d;\ \text{tok } b\to d);\\ I_{6xx} = I_{1xx} &= \dfrac{\varphi_b - \varphi_d + E_1}{R_6+R_1} = \dfrac{20{,}274 - 0 + 80}{16} = 6{,}267\ \text{A} \quad (\text{yo'l } b–c–d;\ \text{tok } b\to d). \end{aligned}

Tekshirish («b» tugun, chiquvchi toklar yig’indisi): (-9,966) + 3,699 + 6,267 = 0,000 ≈ 0.

a va c nuqtalar potensiallarini b tugundan boshlab, tarmoqlar bo’ylab Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni asosida topamiz (har bir rezistorda tok yo’nalishida potensial I·R ga kamayadi, EYuK yo’nalishida E ga ortadi):

φa=φb−I3xxR3+E3=20,274−3,699⋅10+50=33,288V;φc=φb−I6xxR6=20,274−6,267⋅12=−54,932V. \begin{aligned} \varphi_a &= \varphi_b - I_{3xx}R_3 + E_3 = 20{,}274 - 3{,}699\cdot 10 + 50 = 33{,}288\ \text{V};\\ \varphi_c &= \varphi_b - I_{6xx}R_6 = 20{,}274 - 6{,}267\cdot 12 = -54{,}932\ \text{V}. \end{aligned}

Uxx=φa−φc=33,288−(−54,932)=88,219V. U_{xx} = \varphi_a - \varphi_c = 33{,}288 - (-54{,}932) = 88{,}219\ \text{V}.

5.2. Ekvivalent qarshilik RekvR_{ekv} ni aniqlash

Barcha EYuK manbalarini ularning ichki qarshiligi (nol) bilan almashtiramiz va 4-tarmoqni uzilgan holda qoldiramiz (6-rasm). Hosil bo’lgan passiv sxemani a va c qisqichlarga nisbatan bosqichma-bosqich soddalashtiramiz.

a, b, d tugunlar orasidagi R3R_3, R2R_2, R5R_5 qarshiliklar uchburchak hosil qiladi. Uni ekvivalent yulduzga almashtiramiz (yulduz markazi — O nuqta):

Ra=R3⋅R5R3+R2+R5=10⋅927=3,3333Om;Rb=R3⋅R2R3+R2+R5=10⋅827=2,9630Om;Rd=R5⋅R2R3+R2+R5=9⋅827=2,6667Om. \begin{aligned} R_{a} &= \dfrac{R_3\cdot R_5}{R_3 + R_2 + R_5} = \dfrac{10\cdot 9}{27} = 3{,}3333\ \text{Om};\\ R_{b} &= \dfrac{R_3\cdot R_2}{R_3 + R_2 + R_5} = \dfrac{10\cdot 8}{27} = 2{,}9630\ \text{Om};\\ R_{d} &= \dfrac{R_5\cdot R_2}{R_3 + R_2 + R_5} = \dfrac{9\cdot 8}{27} = 2{,}6667\ \text{Om}. \end{aligned}

b nuqta orqali R6R_6 va RbR_{b} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re1=R6+Rb=12+2,9630=14,9630Om.R_{e1} = R_6 + R_{b} = 12 + 2{,}9630 = 14{,}9630\ \text{Om}.

d nuqta orqali R1R_1 va RdR_{d} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re2=R1+Rd=4+2,6667=6,6667Om.R_{e2} = R_1 + R_{d} = 4 + 2{,}6667 = 6{,}6667\ \text{Om}.

c va O nuqtalar orasidagi Re1R_{e1} va Re2R_{e2} qarshiliklar parallel ulangan:

Re3=Re1⋅Re2Re1+Re2=14,9630⋅6,666714,9630+6,6667=4,6119Om.R_{e3} = \dfrac{R_{e1}\cdot R_{e2}}{R_{e1} + R_{e2}} = \dfrac{14{,}9630\cdot 6{,}6667}{14{,}9630 + 6{,}6667} = 4{,}6119\ \text{Om}.

O nuqta orqali RaR_{a} va Re3R_{e3} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re4=Ra+Re3=3,3333+4,6119=7,9452Om.R_{e4} = R_{a} + R_{e3} = 3{,}3333 + 4{,}6119 = 7{,}9452\ \text{Om}.

Natijada: Rekv=7,9452R_{ekv} = 7{,}9452 Om.

I4=UxxR4+Rekv=88,2197+7,9452=88,21914,9452=5,903A. I_4 = \dfrac{U_{xx}}{R_4 + R_{ekv}} = \dfrac{88{,}219}{7 + 7{,}9452} = \dfrac{88{,}219}{14{,}9452} = 5{,}903\ \text{A}.

γ=|I4−I4,eg|I4⋅100%=|5,903−5,903|5,903⋅100%=0,00%. \gamma = \dfrac{|I_4 - I_{4,eg}|}{I_4}\cdot 100\% = \dfrac{|5{,}903 - 5{,}903|}{5{,}903}\cdot 100\% = 0{,}00\%.

Ekvivalent generator usuli natijasi kontur toklar va tugun potensiallar usullari natijalari bilan mos keldi.
