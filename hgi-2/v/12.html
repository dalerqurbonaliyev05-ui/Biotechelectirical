Sxemani tahlil qilish va tok yo’nalishlarini tanlash

Sxemada 4 ta tugun (a, b, c, d) va 6 ta tarmoq bor (n = 4, m = 6). Har bir tarmoq raqami undagi rezistor raqamiga teng:

Tarmoq Tarkibi Ulanish tugunlari Tokning tanlangan yo’nalishi
1 R₁, E₁ b – d d → b
2 R₂, E₂ c – d c → d
3 R₃, E₃ a – b b → a
4 R₄ b – c c → b
5 R₅ a – c a → c
6 R₆ a – d d → a

Kirxgofning I qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: n − 1 = 4 − 1 = 3. Kirxgofning II qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: m − (n − 1) = 6 − 3 = 3. Jami 6 ta tenglama — 6 ta noma’lum tokni topish uchun yetarli.

Tarmoq toklarining shartli musbat yo’nalishlari 2-rasmda qizil strelkalar bilan ko’rsatilgan. Konturlarni aylanib chiqish yo’nalishi — soat mili bo’yicha (yashil strelkalar).

2. Kontur toklar usuli

Kontur toklar usuli tenglamalarining umumiy ko’rinishi:

{I11(R3+R4+R5)−I22R5−I33R4=E3−I11R5+I22(R2+R5+R6)−I33R2=−E2−I11R4−I22R2+I33(R1+R2+R4)=−E1+E2 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &I_{11}(R_3+R_4+R_5) - I_{22}R_5 - I_{33}R_4 = E_3\\ &-I_{11}R_5 + I_{22}(R_2+R_5+R_6) - I_{33}R_2 = -E_2\\ &-I_{11}R_4 - I_{22}R_2 + I_{33}(R_1+R_2+R_4) = -E_1 + E_2 \end{aligned} \right.

5.2. Ekvivalent qarshilik RekvR_{ekv} ni aniqlash

Barcha EYuK manbalarini ularning ichki qarshiligi (nol) bilan almashtiramiz va 4-tarmoqni uzilgan holda qoldiramiz (6-rasm). Hosil bo’lgan passiv sxemani c va b qisqichlarga nisbatan bosqichma-bosqich soddalashtiramiz.

a, b, d tugunlar orasidagi R3R_3, R1R_1, R6R_6 qarshiliklar uchburchak hosil qiladi. Uni ekvivalent yulduzga almashtiramiz (yulduz markazi — O nuqta):

Ra=R3⋅R6R3+R1+R6=10⋅1226=4,6154Om;Rb=R3⋅R1R3+R1+R6=10⋅426=1,5385Om;Rd=R6⋅R1R3+R1+R6=12⋅426=1,8462Om. \begin{aligned} R_{a} &= \dfrac{R_3\cdot R_6}{R_3 + R_1 + R_6} = \dfrac{10\cdot 12}{26} = 4{,}6154\ \text{Om};\\ R_{b} &= \dfrac{R_3\cdot R_1}{R_3 + R_1 + R_6} = \dfrac{10\cdot 4}{26} = 1{,}5385\ \text{Om};\\ R_{d} &= \dfrac{R_6\cdot R_1}{R_3 + R_1 + R_6} = \dfrac{12\cdot 4}{26} = 1{,}8462\ \text{Om}. \end{aligned}

d nuqta orqali R2R_2 va RdR_{d} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re1=R2+Rd=8+1,8462=9,8462Om.R_{e1} = R_2 + R_{d} = 8 + 1{,}8462 = 9{,}8462\ \text{Om}.

a nuqta orqali R5R_5 va RaR_{a} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re2=R5+Ra=9+4,6154=13,6154Om.R_{e2} = R_5 + R_{a} = 9 + 4{,}6154 = 13{,}6154\ \text{Om}.

c va O nuqtalar orasidagi Re1R_{e1} va Re2R_{e2} qarshiliklar parallel ulangan:

Re3=Re1⋅Re2Re1+Re2=9,8462⋅13,61549,8462+13,6154=5,7140Om.R_{e3} = \dfrac{R_{e1}\cdot R_{e2}}{R_{e1} + R_{e2}} = \dfrac{9{,}8462\cdot 13{,}6154}{9{,}8462 + 13{,}6154} = 5{,}7140\ \text{Om}.

O nuqta orqali RbR_{b} va Re3R_{e3} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re4=Rb+Re3=1,5385+5,7140=7,2525Om.R_{e4} = R_{b} + R_{e3} = 1{,}5385 + 5{,}7140 = 7{,}2525\ \text{Om}.

6. Quvvat muvozanati

Manbalar quvvati: EYuK yo’nalishi tok yo’nalishiga mos kelsa, manba energiya beradi (E·I «+» ishora bilan), teskari bo’lsa — energiya iste’mol qiladi («−» ishora bilan). 1-tarmoqda I₁ toki E₁ ga teskari yo’nalgan, shuning uchun E₁ manbai iste’molchi rejimida ishlaydi.

Pmanba=−E1I1+E2I2+E3I3=−80⋅1,594+100⋅3,478+100⋅3,623=−127,52+347,80+362,30=582,58Vt. P_{manba} = -E_1I_1 + E_2I_2 + E_3I_3 = -80\cdot 1{,}594 + 100\cdot 3{,}478 + 100\cdot 3{,}623 = -127{,}52 + 347{,}80 + 362{,}30 = 582{,}58\ \text{Vt}.

Iste’molchilar (rezistorlar) quvvati:

Pist=I12R1+I22R2+I32R3+I42R4+I52R5+I62R6==1,5942⋅4+3,4782⋅8+3,6232⋅10+2,0292⋅7+5,5072⋅9+1,8842⋅12==10,163+96,772+131,261+28,818+272,943+42,593=582,551Vt. \begin{aligned} P_{ist} &= I_1^2R_1 + I_2^2R_2 + I_3^2R_3 + I_4^2R_4 + I_5^2R_5 + I_6^2R_6 =\\ &= 1{,}594^2\cdot 4 + 3{,}478^2\cdot 8 + 3{,}623^2\cdot 10 + 2{,}029^2\cdot 7 + 5{,}507^2\cdot 9 + 1{,}884^2\cdot 12 =\\ &= 10{,}163 + 96{,}772 + 131{,}261 + 28{,}818 + 272{,}943 + 42{,}593 = 582{,}551\ \text{Vt}. \end{aligned}

Hisoblash xatoligi:

γ=|Pmanba−Pist|Pmanba⋅100%=|582,58−582,551|582,58⋅100%=0,0049%. \gamma = \dfrac{|P_{manba} - P_{ist}|}{P_{manba}}\cdot 100\% = \dfrac{|582{,}58 - 582{,}551|}{582{,}58}\cdot 100\% = 0{,}0049\%.
