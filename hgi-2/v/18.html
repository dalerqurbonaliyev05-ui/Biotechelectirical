Topshiriq. Berilgan sxema uchun:

  1. Kirxgofning birinchi va ikkinchi qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish;
  2. kontur toklar usuli bo’yicha tarmoq toklarini topish;
  3. tugun potensiallar usuli bo’yicha tarmoq toklarini topish;
  4. topilgan toklarni Kirxgofning I va II qonunlari asosida tekshirish;
  5. ekvivalent generator usuli yordamida 2-tarmoqdagi tokni (I₂) aniqlash;

Sxemani tahlil qilish va tok yo’nalishlarini tanlash

Sxemada 4 ta tugun (a, b, c, d) va 6 ta tarmoq bor (n = 4, m = 6). Har bir tarmoq raqami undagi rezistor raqamiga teng:

Tarmoq Tarkibi Ulanish tugunlari Tokning tanlangan yo’nalishi
1 R₁, E₁ a – c c → a
2 R₂, E₂ b – c b → c
3 R₃, E₃ b – d b → d
4 R₄ a – b a → b
5 R₅ a – d d → a
6 R₆ c – d d → c

Kirxgofning I qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: n − 1 = 4 − 1 = 3. Kirxgofning II qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: m − (n − 1) = 6 − 3 = 3. Jami 6 ta tenglama — 6 ta noma’lum tokni topish uchun yetarli.

Tarmoq toklarining shartli musbat yo’nalishlari 2-rasmda qizil strelkalar bilan ko’rsatilgan. Konturlarni aylanib chiqish yo’nalishi — soat mili bo’yicha (yashil strelkalar).

1. Kirxgof qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish

Kirxgofning birinchi qonuni (tugunga kiruvchi toklar «+», chiquvchi toklar «−» ishora bilan olinadi):

«a» tugun uchun:I1−I4+I5=0;«b» tugun uchun:−I2−I3+I4=0;«c» tugun uchun:−I1+I2+I6=0;«d» tugun uchun:I3−I5−I6=0.(tekshirish uchun) \begin{aligned} &\text{«a» tugun uchun:} && I_1 - I_4 + I_5 = 0;\\ &\text{«b» tugun uchun:} && -I_2 - I_3 + I_4 = 0;\\ &\text{«c» tugun uchun:} && -I_1 + I_2 + I_6 = 0;\\ &\text{«d» tugun uchun:} && I_3 - I_5 - I_6 = 0. \quad (\text{tekshirish uchun}) \end{aligned}

Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni (tok yoki EYuK yo’nalishi kontur aylanish yo’nalishiga mos kelsa «+», teskari bo’lsa «−»):

I kontur (c–b–a–c):−I2R2−I4R4−I1R1=−E2−E1;II kontur (d–a–b–d):I5R5+I4R4+I3R3=E3;III kontur (c–d–b–c):−I6R6−I3R3+I2R2=−E3+E2. \begin{aligned} &\text{I kontur (c–b–a–c):} && -I_2R_2 - I_4R_4 - I_1R_1 = -E_2 - E_1;\\ &\text{II kontur (d–a–b–d):} && I_5R_5 + I_4R_4 + I_3R_3 = E_3;\\ &\text{III kontur (c–d–b–c):} && -I_6R_6 - I_3R_3 + I_2R_2 = -E_3 + E_2. \end{aligned}

Son qiymatlarini qo’ysak:

{I1−I4+I5=0−I2−I3+I4=0−I1+I2+I6=0−2I2−3I4−6I1=−15015I5+3I4+4I3=75−5I6−4I3+2I2=−25 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &I_1 - I_4 + I_5 = 0\\ &-I_2 - I_3 + I_4 = 0\\ &-I_1 + I_2 + I_6 = 0\\ &-2I_2 - 3I_4 - 6I_1 = -150\\ &15I_5 + 3I_4 + 4I_3 = 75\\ &-5I_6 - 4I_3 + 2I_2 = -25 \end{aligned} \right.

Bu 6 noma’lumli tenglamalar sistemasining yechimi quyida kontur toklar va tugun potensiallar usullari yordamida topiladi.

2. Kontur toklar usuli

Kontur toklarini I₁₁, I₂₂, I₃₃ bilan belgilab, ularni soat mili yo’nalishida yo’naltiramiz (3-rasm).

Kontur toklar usuli tenglamalarining umumiy ko’rinishi:

{I11(R1+R2+R4)−I22R4−I33R2=−E1−E2−I11R4+I22(R3+R4+R5)−I33R3=E3−I11R2−I22R3+I33(R2+R3+R6)=E2−E3 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &I_{11}(R_1+R_2+R_4) - I_{22}R_4 - I_{33}R_2 = -E_1 - E_2\\ &-I_{11}R_4 + I_{22}(R_3+R_4+R_5) - I_{33}R_3 = E_3\\ &-I_{11}R_2 - I_{22}R_3 + I_{33}(R_2+R_3+R_6) = E_2 - E_3 \end{aligned} \right.

Bu yerda: R₁₁ = R₁ + R₂ + R₄ = 6 + 2 + 3 = 11 Om; R₂₂ = R₃ + R₄ + R₅ = 4 + 3 + 15 = 22 Om; R₃₃ = R₂ + R₃ + R₆ = 2 + 4 + 5 = 11 Om — konturlarning xususiy qarshiliklari; R₁₂ = R₄ = 3 Om, R₁₃ = R₂ = 2 Om, R₂₃ = R₃ = 4 Om — qo’shni konturlarning umumiy qarshiliklari (kontur toklari umumiy tarmoqda qarama-qarshi yo’nalgani uchun tenglamada «−» ishora bilan olinadi). Konturlarning EYuKlari (aylanish yo’nalishiga mos EYuK «+», teskari EYuK «−»): E₁₁ = −E₁ − E₂ = -150 V; E₂₂ = E₃ = 75 V; E₃₃ = E₂ − E₃ = -25 V.

Son qiymatlarini qo’yamiz:

{11I11−3I22−2I33=−150−3I11+22I22−4I33=75−2I11−4I22+11I33=−25 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &11\,I_{11} - 3\,I_{22} - 2\,I_{33} = -150\\ &-3\,I_{11} + 22\,I_{22} - 4\,I_{33} = 75\\ &-2\,I_{11} - 4\,I_{22} + 11\,I_{33} = -25 \end{aligned} \right.

Tenglamalar sistemasini Kramer usuli bilan yechamiz. Bosh determinant:

Δ=∣11−3−2−322−4−2−411∣=2251.\Delta = \begin{vmatrix} 11 & -3 & -2 \\ -3 & 22 & -4 \\ -2 & -4 & 11 \end{vmatrix} = 2251.

Yordamchi determinantlar (tegishli ustun ozod hadlar bilan almashtiriladi):

Δ11=∣−150−3−27522−4−25−411∣=−32225.\Delta_{11} = \begin{vmatrix} -150 & -3 & -2 \\ 75 & 22 & -4 \\ -25 & -4 & 11 \end{vmatrix} = -32225.

Δ22=∣11−150−2−375−4−2−2511∣=1375.\Delta_{22} = \begin{vmatrix} 11 & -150 & -2 \\ -3 & 75 & -4 \\ -2 & -25 & 11 \end{vmatrix} = 1375.

Δ33=∣11−3−150−32275−2−4−25∣=−10475.\Delta_{33} = \begin{vmatrix} 11 & -3 & -150 \\ -3 & 22 & 75 \\ -2 & -4 & -25 \end{vmatrix} = -10475.

I₁₁, I₃₃ larning manfiy chiqishi bu kontur toklarining haqiqiy yo’nalishi soat miliga teskari ekanini bildiradi.

Kontur toklari orqali tarmoq toklarini aniqlaymiz (tarmoq tokining yo’nalishi bilan mos kontur toki «+», teskari kontur toki «−» bilan olinadi):

I1=−I11=−(−14,316)=14,316A;I2=−I11+I33=−(−14,316)+(−4,653)=9,662A;I3=I22−I33=0,611−(−4,653)=5,264A;I4=−I11+I22=−(−14,316)+0,611=14,927A;I5=I22=0,611A;I6=−I33=−(−4,653)=4,653A. \begin{aligned} I_1 &= -I_{11} = -(-14{,}316) = 14{,}316\ \text{A};\\ I_2 &= -I_{11} + I_{33} = -(-14{,}316) + (-4{,}653) = 9{,}662\ \text{A};\\ I_3 &= I_{22} - I_{33} = 0{,}611 - (-4{,}653) = 5{,}264\ \text{A};\\ I_4 &= -I_{11} + I_{22} = -(-14{,}316) + 0{,}611 = 14{,}927\ \text{A};\\ I_5 &= I_{22} = 0{,}611\ \text{A};\\ I_6 &= -I_{33} = -(-4{,}653) = 4{,}653\ \text{A}. \end{aligned}

Barcha tarmoq toklari musbat chiqdi, demak 2-rasmda tanlangan yo’nalishlar toklarning haqiqiy yo’nalishlari bilan mos keladi.

4. Toklarni Kirxgof qonunlari bo’yicha tekshirish

Kirxgofning birinchi qonuni bo’yicha:

Barcha tenglamalar bajarildi (farq faqat toklarni uch xonagacha yaxlitlash hisobiga), demak toklar to’g’ri topilgan.

5. Ekvivalent generator usuli bilan I₂ tokini aniqlash

Ekvivalent generator usuliga ko’ra 2-tarmoqqa nisbatan qolgan barcha zanjir EYuKi Eekv=UxxE_{ekv} = U_{xx} va ichki qarshiligi RekvR_{ekv} bo’lgan bitta ekvivalent manba bilan almashtiriladi (7-rasm). Shunda:

I2=Uxx+E2R2+Rekv,I_2 = \dfrac{U_{xx} + E_2}{R_2 + R_{ekv}},

bu yerda Uxx=φb−φcU_{xx} = \varphi_b - \varphi_c — 2-tarmoq uzilgandagi (salt ishlash rejimidagi) b va c tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish; RekvR_{ekv} — EYuKlar qisqa tutashtirilgandagi zanjirning b va c qisqichlariga nisbatan kirish qarshiligi.

5.1. Salt ishlash kuchlanishi UxxU_{xx} ni aniqlash

2-tarmoq uzilganda (5-rasm) b va c nuqtalar tugun bo’lmay qoladi va ular orqali o’tgan tarmoqlar ketma-ket ulanadi. Natijada zanjir a va d tugunlar orasida parallel ulangan uchta tarmoqdan iborat bo’ladi:

  • a–c–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₁ + R₆ = 6 + 5 = 11 Om, a tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK E₁ = 100 V;
  • a–b–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₄ + R₃ = 3 + 4 = 7 Om, a tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK −E₃ = -75 V;
  • a–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₅ = 15 Om, EYuK yo’q;

Ikki tugun usuli bo’yicha a va d tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish (φd=0\varphi_d = 0):

Uad=φa=E1R1+R6+−E3R4+R31R1+R6+1R4+R3+1R5=10011−757111+17+115=−1,62340,3004=−5,403V. U_{ad} = \varphi_a = \dfrac{\dfrac{E_1}{R_1+R_6} + \dfrac{-E_3}{R_4+R_3}}{\dfrac{1}{R_1+R_6}+\dfrac{1}{R_4+R_3}+\dfrac{1}{R_5}} = \dfrac{\dfrac{100}{11} - \dfrac{75}{7}}{\dfrac{1}{11}+\dfrac{1}{7}+\dfrac{1}{15}} = \dfrac{-1{,}6234}{0{,}3004} = -5{,}403\ \text{V}.

Salt ishlash rejimidagi tarmoq toklari (a tugundan d tugunga qarab hisoblanadi; manfiy qiymat tok aslida teskari yo’nalganini bildiradi):

b va c nuqtalar potensiallarini a tugundan boshlab, tarmoqlar bo’ylab Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni asosida topamiz (har bir rezistorda tok yo’nalishida potensial I·R ga kamayadi, EYuK yo’nalishida E ga ortadi):

φb=φa−I4xxR4=(−5,403)−9,942⋅3=−35,231V;φc=φa−I1xxR1−E1=(−5,403)−(−9,582)⋅6−100=−47,911V. \begin{aligned} \varphi_b &= \varphi_a - I_{4xx}R_4 = (-5{,}403) - 9{,}942\cdot 3 = -35{,}231\ \text{V};\\ \varphi_c &= \varphi_a - I_{1xx}R_1 - E_1 = (-5{,}403) - (-9{,}582)\cdot 6 - 100 = -47{,}911\ \text{V}. \end{aligned}

5.2. Ekvivalent qarshilik RekvR_{ekv} ni aniqlash

Barcha EYuK manbalarini ularning ichki qarshiligi (nol) bilan almashtiramiz va 2-tarmoqni uzilgan holda qoldiramiz (6-rasm). Hosil bo’lgan passiv sxemani b va c qisqichlarga nisbatan bosqichma-bosqich soddalashtiramiz.

a, b, d tugunlar orasidagi R4R_4, R3R_3, R5R_5 qarshiliklar uchburchak hosil qiladi. Uni ekvivalent yulduzga almashtiramiz (yulduz markazi — O nuqta):

Ra=R4⋅R5R4+R3+R5=3⋅1522=2,0455Om;Rb=R4⋅R3R4+R3+R5=3⋅422=0,5455Om;Rd=R5⋅R3R4+R3+R5=15⋅422=2,7273Om. \begin{aligned} R_{a} &= \dfrac{R_4\cdot R_5}{R_4 + R_3 + R_5} = \dfrac{3\cdot 15}{22} = 2{,}0455\ \text{Om};\\ R_{b} &= \dfrac{R_4\cdot R_3}{R_4 + R_3 + R_5} = \dfrac{3\cdot 4}{22} = 0{,}5455\ \text{Om};\\ R_{d} &= \dfrac{R_5\cdot R_3}{R_4 + R_3 + R_5} = \dfrac{15\cdot 4}{22} = 2{,}7273\ \text{Om}. \end{aligned}

d nuqta orqali R6R_6 va RdR_{d} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re1=R6+Rd=5+2,7273=7,7273Om.R_{e1} = R_6 + R_{d} = 5 + 2{,}7273 = 7{,}7273\ \text{Om}.

a nuqta orqali R1R_1 va RaR_{a} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

c va O nuqtalar orasidagi Re1R_{e1} va Re2R_{e2} qarshiliklar parallel ulangan:

Re3=Re1⋅Re2Re1+Re2=7,7273⋅8,04557,7273+8,0455=3,9416Om.R_{e3} = \dfrac{R_{e1}\cdot R_{e2}}{R_{e1} + R_{e2}} = \dfrac{7{,}7273\cdot 8{,}0455}{7{,}7273 + 8{,}0455} = 3{,}9416\ \text{Om}.

O nuqta orqali RbR_{b} va Re3R_{e3} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:
