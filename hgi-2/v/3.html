Topshiriq. Berilgan sxema uchun:

  1. Kirxgofning birinchi va ikkinchi qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish;
  2. kontur toklar usuli bo’yicha tarmoq toklarini topish;
  3. tugun potensiallar usuli bo’yicha tarmoq toklarini topish;
  4. topilgan toklarni Kirxgofning I va II qonunlari asosida tekshirish;

Sxemani tahlil qilish va tok yo’nalishlarini tanlash

Sxemada 4 ta tugun (a, b, c, d) va 6 ta tarmoq bor (n = 4, m = 6). Har bir tarmoq raqami undagi rezistor raqamiga teng:

Tarmoq Tarkibi Ulanish tugunlari Tokning tanlangan yo’nalishi
1 R₁, E₁ b – d d → b
2 R₂, E₂ c – d c → d
3 R₃, E₃ b – c b → c
4 R₄ a – b a → b
5 R₅ a – c c → a
6 R₆ a – d d → a

Kirxgofning I qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: n − 1 = 4 − 1 = 3. Kirxgofning II qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: m − (n − 1) = 6 − 3 = 3. Jami 6 ta tenglama — 6 ta noma’lum tokni topish uchun yetarli.

Tarmoq toklarining shartli musbat yo’nalishlari 2-rasmda qizil strelkalar bilan ko’rsatilgan. Konturlarni aylanib chiqish yo’nalishi — soat mili bo’yicha (yashil strelkalar).

1. Kirxgof qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish

Kirxgofning birinchi qonuni (tugunga kiruvchi toklar «+», chiquvchi toklar «−» ishora bilan olinadi):

«a» tugun uchun:−I4+I5+I6=0;«b» tugun uchun:I1−I3+I4=0;«c» tugun uchun:−I2+I3−I5=0;«d» tugun uchun:−I1+I2−I6=0.(tekshirish uchun) \begin{aligned} &\text{«a» tugun uchun:} && -I_4 + I_5 + I_6 = 0;\\ &\text{«b» tugun uchun:} && I_1 - I_3 + I_4 = 0;\\ &\text{«c» tugun uchun:} && -I_2 + I_3 - I_5 = 0;\\ &\text{«d» tugun uchun:} && -I_1 + I_2 - I_6 = 0. \quad (\text{tekshirish uchun}) \end{aligned}

Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni (tok yoki EYuK yo’nalishi kontur aylanish yo’nalishiga mos kelsa «+», teskari bo’lsa «−»):

I kontur (b–a–c–b):−I4R4−I5R5−I3R3=−E3;II kontur (c–a–d–c):I5R5−I6R6−I2R2=−E2;III kontur (b–c–d–b):I3R3+I2R2+I1R1=E3+E2−E1. \begin{aligned} &\text{I kontur (b–a–c–b):} && -I_4R_4 - I_5R_5 - I_3R_3 = -E_3;\\ &\text{II kontur (c–a–d–c):} && I_5R_5 - I_6R_6 - I_2R_2 = -E_2;\\ &\text{III kontur (b–c–d–b):} && I_3R_3 + I_2R_2 + I_1R_1 = E_3 + E_2 - E_1. \end{aligned}

Son qiymatlarini qo’ysak:

{−I4+I5+I6=0I1−I3+I4=0−I2+I3−I5=0−8I4−6I5−5I3=−1006I5−10I6−4I2=−905I3+4I2+12I1=110 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &-I_4 + I_5 + I_6 = 0\\ &I_1 - I_3 + I_4 = 0\\ &-I_2 + I_3 - I_5 = 0\\ &-8I_4 - 6I_5 - 5I_3 = -100\\ &6I_5 - 10I_6 - 4I_2 = -90\\ &5I_3 + 4I_2 + 12I_1 = 110 \end{aligned} \right.

Bu 6 noma’lumli tenglamalar sistemasining yechimi quyida kontur toklar va tugun potensiallar usullari yordamida topiladi.

2. Kontur toklar usuli

Kontur toklarini I₁₁, I₂₂, I₃₃ bilan belgilab, ularni soat mili yo’nalishida yo’naltiramiz (3-rasm).

Kontur toklar usuli tenglamalarining umumiy ko’rinishi:

{I11(R3+R4+R5)−I22R5−I33R3=−E3−I11R5+I22(R2+R5+R6)−I33R2=−E2−I11R3−I22R2+I33(R1+R2+R3)=−E1+E2+E3 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &I_{11}(R_3+R_4+R_5) - I_{22}R_5 - I_{33}R_3 = -E_3\\ &-I_{11}R_5 + I_{22}(R_2+R_5+R_6) - I_{33}R_2 = -E_2\\ &-I_{11}R_3 - I_{22}R_2 + I_{33}(R_1+R_2+R_3) = -E_1 + E_2 + E_3 \end{aligned} \right.

Bu yerda: R₁₁ = R₃ + R₄ + R₅ = 5 + 8 + 6 = 19 Om; R₂₂ = R₂ + R₅ + R₆ = 4 + 6 + 10 = 20 Om; R₃₃ = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ = 12 + 4 + 5 = 21 Om — konturlarning xususiy qarshiliklari; R₁₂ = R₅ = 6 Om, R₁₃ = R₃ = 5 Om, R₂₃ = R₂ = 4 Om — qo’shni konturlarning umumiy qarshiliklari (kontur toklari umumiy tarmoqda qarama-qarshi yo’nalgani uchun tenglamada «−» ishora bilan olinadi). Konturlarning EYuKlari (aylanish yo’nalishiga mos EYuK «+», teskari EYuK «−»): E₁₁ = −E₃ = -100 V; E₂₂ = −E₂ = -90 V; E₃₃ = −E₁ + E₂ + E₃ = 110 V.

Son qiymatlarini qo’yamiz:

{19I11−6I22−5I33=−100−6I11+20I22−4I33=−90−5I11−4I22+21I33=110 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &19\,I_{11} - 6\,I_{22} - 5\,I_{33} = -100\\ &-6\,I_{11} + 20\,I_{22} - 4\,I_{33} = -90\\ &-5\,I_{11} - 4\,I_{22} + 21\,I_{33} = 110 \end{aligned} \right.

Tenglamalar sistemasini Kramer usuli bilan yechamiz. Bosh determinant:

Δ=∣19−6−5−620−4−5−421∣=6180.\Delta = \begin{vmatrix} 19 & -6 & -5 \\ -6 & 20 & -4 \\ -5 & -4 & 21 \end{vmatrix} = 6180.

Yordamchi determinantlar (tegishli ustun ozod hadlar bilan almashtiriladi):

Δ11=∣−100−6−5−9020−4110−421∣=−39900.\Delta_{11} = \begin{vmatrix} -100 & -6 & -5 \\ -90 & 20 & -4 \\ 110 & -4 & 21 \end{vmatrix} = -39900.

Δ22=∣19−100−5−6−90−4−511021∣=−36600.\Delta_{22} = \begin{vmatrix} 19 & -100 & -5 \\ -6 & -90 & -4 \\ -5 & 110 & 21 \end{vmatrix} = -36600.

Δ33=∣19−6−100−620−90−5−4110∣=15900.\Delta_{33} = \begin{vmatrix} 19 & -6 & -100 \\ -6 & 20 & -90 \\ -5 & -4 & 110 \end{vmatrix} = 15900.

I₁₁, I₂₂ larning manfiy chiqishi bu kontur toklarining haqiqiy yo’nalishi soat miliga teskari ekanini bildiradi.

Kontur toklari orqali tarmoq toklarini aniqlaymiz (tarmoq tokining yo’nalishi bilan mos kontur toki «+», teskari kontur toki «−» bilan olinadi):

I1=I33=2,573A;I2=−I22+I33=−(−5,922)+2,573=8,495A;I3=−I11+I33=−(−6,456)+2,573=9,029A;I4=−I11=−(−6,456)=6,456A;I5=−I11+I22=−(−6,456)+(−5,922)=0,534A;I6=−I22=−(−5,922)=5,922A. \begin{aligned} I_1 &= I_{33} = 2{,}573\ \text{A};\\ I_2 &= -I_{22} + I_{33} = -(-5{,}922) + 2{,}573 = 8{,}495\ \text{A};\\ I_3 &= -I_{11} + I_{33} = -(-6{,}456) + 2{,}573 = 9{,}029\ \text{A};\\ I_4 &= -I_{11} = -(-6{,}456) = 6{,}456\ \text{A};\\ I_5 &= -I_{11} + I_{22} = -(-6{,}456) + (-5{,}922) = 0{,}534\ \text{A};\\ I_6 &= -I_{22} = -(-5{,}922) = 5{,}922\ \text{A}. \end{aligned}

Barcha tarmoq toklari musbat chiqdi, demak 2-rasmda tanlangan yo’nalishlar toklarning haqiqiy yo’nalishlari bilan mos keladi.

5. Ekvivalent generator usuli bilan I₃ tokini aniqlash

Ekvivalent generator usuliga ko’ra 3-tarmoqqa nisbatan qolgan barcha zanjir EYuKi Eekv=UxxE_{ekv} = U_{xx} va ichki qarshiligi RekvR_{ekv} bo’lgan bitta ekvivalent manba bilan almashtiriladi (7-rasm). Shunda:

I3=Uxx+E3R3+Rekv,I_3 = \dfrac{U_{xx} + E_3}{R_3 + R_{ekv}},

bu yerda Uxx=φb−φcU_{xx} = \varphi_b - \varphi_c — 3-tarmoq uzilgandagi (salt ishlash rejimidagi) b va c tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish; RekvR_{ekv} — EYuKlar qisqa tutashtirilgandagi zanjirning b va c qisqichlariga nisbatan kirish qarshiligi.

5.1. Salt ishlash kuchlanishi UxxU_{xx} ni aniqlash

3-tarmoq uzilganda (5-rasm) b va c nuqtalar tugun bo’lmay qoladi va ular orqali o’tgan tarmoqlar ketma-ket ulanadi. Natijada zanjir a va d tugunlar orasida parallel ulangan uchta tarmoqdan iborat bo’ladi:

  • a–b–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₄ + R₁ = 8 + 12 = 20 Om, a tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK −E₁ = -80 V;
  • a–c–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₅ + R₂ = 6 + 4 = 10 Om, a tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK −E₂ = -90 V;
  • a–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₆ = 10 Om, EYuK yo’q;

Ikki tugun usuli bo’yicha a va d tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish (φd=0\varphi_d = 0):

Uad=φa=−E1R4+R1+−E2R5+R21R4+R1+1R5+R2+1R6=−8020−9010120+110+110=−130,25=−52V. U_{ad} = \varphi_a = \dfrac{\dfrac{-E_1}{R_4+R_1} + \dfrac{-E_2}{R_5+R_2}}{\dfrac{1}{R_4+R_1}+\dfrac{1}{R_5+R_2}+\dfrac{1}{R_6}} = \dfrac{-\dfrac{80}{20} - \dfrac{90}{10}}{\dfrac{1}{20}+\dfrac{1}{10}+\dfrac{1}{10}} = \dfrac{-13}{0{,}25} = -52\ \text{V}.

Salt ishlash rejimidagi tarmoq toklari (a tugundan d tugunga qarab hisoblanadi; manfiy qiymat tok aslida teskari yo’nalganini bildiradi):

Tekshirish («a» tugun, chiquvchi toklar yig’indisi): 1,400 + 3,800 + (-5,200) = 0,000 ≈ 0.

b va c nuqtalar potensiallarini a tugundan boshlab, tarmoqlar bo’ylab Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni asosida topamiz (har bir rezistorda tok yo’nalishida potensial I·R ga kamayadi, EYuK yo’nalishida E ga ortadi):

φb=φa−I4xxR4=(−52)−1,4⋅8=−63,2V;φc=φa−I5xxR5=(−52)−3,8⋅6=−74,8V. \begin{aligned} \varphi_b &= \varphi_a - I_{4xx}R_4 = (-52) - 1{,}4\cdot 8 = -63{,}2\ \text{V};\\ \varphi_c &= \varphi_a - I_{5xx}R_5 = (-52) - 3{,}8\cdot 6 = -74{,}8\ \text{V}. \end{aligned}

Uxx=φb−φc=(−63,2)−(−74,8)=11,6V. U_{xx} = \varphi_b - \varphi_c = (-63{,}2) - (-74{,}8) = 11{,}6\ \text{V}.

5.2. Ekvivalent qarshilik RekvR_{ekv} ni aniqlash

Barcha EYuK manbalarini ularning ichki qarshiligi (nol) bilan almashtiramiz va 3-tarmoqni uzilgan holda qoldiramiz (6-rasm). Hosil bo’lgan passiv sxemani b va c qisqichlarga nisbatan bosqichma-bosqich soddalashtiramiz.

a, b, d tugunlar orasidagi R4R_4, R1R_1, R6R_6 qarshiliklar uchburchak hosil qiladi. Uni ekvivalent yulduzga almashtiramiz (yulduz markazi — O nuqta):

Ra=R4⋅R6R4+R1+R6=8⋅1030=2,6667Om;Rb=R4⋅R1R4+R1+R6=8⋅1230=3,2Om;Rd=R6⋅R1R4+R1+R6=10⋅1230=4Om. \begin{aligned} R_{a} &= \dfrac{R_4\cdot R_6}{R_4 + R_1 + R_6} = \dfrac{8\cdot 10}{30} = 2{,}6667\ \text{Om};\\ R_{b} &= \dfrac{R_4\cdot R_1}{R_4 + R_1 + R_6} = \dfrac{8\cdot 12}{30} = 3{,}2\ \text{Om};\\ R_{d} &= \dfrac{R_6\cdot R_1}{R_4 + R_1 + R_6} = \dfrac{10\cdot 12}{30} = 4\ \text{Om}. \end{aligned}

d nuqta orqali R2R_2 va RdR_{d} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re1=R2+Rd=4+4=8Om.R_{e1} = R_2 + R_{d} = 4 + 4 = 8\ \text{Om}.

a nuqta orqali R5R_5 va RaR_{a} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re2=R5+Ra=6+2,6667=8,6667Om.R_{e2} = R_5 + R_{a} = 6 + 2{,}6667 = 8{,}6667\ \text{Om}.

c va O nuqtalar orasidagi Re1R_{e1} va Re2R_{e2} qarshiliklar parallel ulangan:

Re3=Re1⋅Re2Re1+Re2=8⋅8,66678+8,6667=4,16Om.R_{e3} = \dfrac{R_{e1}\cdot R_{e2}}{R_{e1} + R_{e2}} = \dfrac{8\cdot 8{,}6667}{8 + 8{,}6667} = 4{,}16\ \text{Om}.

O nuqta orqali RbR_{b} va Re3R_{e3} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re4=Rb+Re3=3,2+4,16=7,36Om.R_{e4} = R_{b} + R_{e3} = 3{,}2 + 4{,}16 = 7{,}36\ \text{Om}.

Natijada: Rekv=7,36R_{ekv} = 7{,}36 Om.

5.3. I₃ tokini hisoblash

I3=Uxx+E3R3+Rekv=11,6+1005+7,36=111,612,36=9,029A. I_3 = \dfrac{U_{xx} + E_3}{R_3 + R_{ekv}} = \dfrac{11{,}6 + 100}{5 + 7{,}36} = \dfrac{111{,}6}{12{,}36} = 9{,}029\ \text{A}.

Kontur toklar usulida topilgan qiymat bilan solishtiramiz:

Ekvivalent generator usuli natijasi kontur toklar va tugun potensiallar usullari natijalari bilan mos keldi.

6. Quvvat muvozanati

Manbalar quvvati: EYuK yo’nalishi tok yo’nalishiga mos kelsa, manba energiya beradi (E·I «+» ishora bilan), teskari bo’lsa — energiya iste’mol qiladi («−» ishora bilan). 1-tarmoqda I₁ toki E₁ ga teskari yo’nalgan, shuning uchun E₁ manbai iste’molchi rejimida ishlaydi.

Pmanba=−E1I1+E2I2+E3I3=−80⋅2,573+90⋅8,495+100⋅9,029=−205,84+764,55+902,90=1461,61Vt. P_{manba} = -E_1I_1 + E_2I_2 + E_3I_3 = -80\cdot 2{,}573 + 90\cdot 8{,}495 + 100\cdot 9{,}029 = -205{,}84 + 764{,}55 + 902{,}90 = 1461{,}61\ \text{Vt}.

Iste’molchilar (rezistorlar) quvvati:

Pist=I12R1+I22R2+I32R3+I42R4+I52R5+I62R6==2,5732⋅12+8,4952⋅4+9,0292⋅5+6,4562⋅8+0,5342⋅6+5,9222⋅10==79,444+288,660+407,614+333,439+1,711+350,701=1461,570Vt. \begin{aligned} P_{ist} &= I_1^2R_1 + I_2^2R_2 + I_3^2R_3 + I_4^2R_4 + I_5^2R_5 + I_6^2R_6 =\\ &= 2{,}573^2\cdot 12 + 8{,}495^2\cdot 4 + 9{,}029^2\cdot 5 + 6{,}456^2\cdot 8 + 0{,}534^2\cdot 6 + 5{,}922^2\cdot 10 =\\ &= 79{,}444 + 288{,}660 + 407{,}614 + 333{,}439 + 1{,}711 + 350{,}701 = 1461{,}570\ \text{Vt}. \end{aligned}

Hisoblash xatoligi:

γ=|Pmanba−Pist|Pmanba⋅100%=|1461,61−1461,570|1461,61⋅100%=0,0028%. \gamma = \dfrac{|P_{manba} - P_{ist}|}{P_{manba}}\cdot 100\% = \dfrac{|1461{,}61 - 1461{,}570|}{1461{,}61}\cdot 100\% = 0{,}0028\%.
