Sxemani tahlil qilish va tok yo’nalishlarini tanlash

Sxemada 4 ta tugun (a, b, c, d) va 6 ta tarmoq bor (n = 4, m = 6). Har bir tarmoq raqami undagi rezistor raqamiga teng:

Tarmoq Tarkibi Ulanish tugunlari Tokning tanlangan yo’nalishi
1 R₁, E₁ a – d d → a
2 R₂, E₂ b – d d → b
3 R₃, E₃ a – b b → a
4 R₄ a – c a → c
5 R₅ b – c b → c
6 R₆ c – d c → d

Kirxgofning I qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: n − 1 = 4 − 1 = 3. Kirxgofning II qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: m − (n − 1) = 6 − 3 = 3. Jami 6 ta tenglama — 6 ta noma’lum tokni topish uchun yetarli.

Tarmoq toklarining shartli musbat yo’nalishlari 2-rasmda qizil strelkalar bilan ko’rsatilgan. Konturlarni aylanib chiqish yo’nalishi — soat mili bo’yicha (yashil strelkalar).

1. Kirxgof qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish

Kirxgofning birinchi qonuni (tugunga kiruvchi toklar «+», chiquvchi toklar «−» ishora bilan olinadi):

«a» tugun uchun:I1+I3−I4=0;«b» tugun uchun:I2−I3−I5=0;«c» tugun uchun:I4+I5−I6=0;«d» tugun uchun:−I1−I2+I6=0.(tekshirish uchun) \begin{aligned} &\text{«a» tugun uchun:} && I_1 + I_3 - I_4 = 0;\\ &\text{«b» tugun uchun:} && I_2 - I_3 - I_5 = 0;\\ &\text{«c» tugun uchun:} && I_4 + I_5 - I_6 = 0;\\ &\text{«d» tugun uchun:} && -I_1 - I_2 + I_6 = 0. \quad (\text{tekshirish uchun}) \end{aligned}

Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni (tok yoki EYuK yo’nalishi kontur aylanish yo’nalishiga mos kelsa «+», teskari bo’lsa «−»):

I kontur (a–c–b–a):I4R4−I5R5+I3R3=E3;II kontur (a–b–d–a):−I3R3−I2R2+I1R1=−E3−E2+E1;III kontur (b–c–d–b):I5R5+I6R6+I2R2=E2. \begin{aligned} &\text{I kontur (a–c–b–a):} && I_4R_4 - I_5R_5 + I_3R_3 = E_3;\\ &\text{II kontur (a–b–d–a):} && -I_3R_3 - I_2R_2 + I_1R_1 = -E_3 - E_2 + E_1;\\ &\text{III kontur (b–c–d–b):} && I_5R_5 + I_6R_6 + I_2R_2 = E_2. \end{aligned}

Son qiymatlarini qo’ysak:

{I1+I3−I4=0I2−I3−I5=0I4+I5−I6=010I4−12I5+6I3=20−6I3−8I2+5I1=012I5+4I6+8I2=40 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &I_1 + I_3 - I_4 = 0\\ &I_2 - I_3 - I_5 = 0\\ &I_4 + I_5 - I_6 = 0\\ &10I_4 - 12I_5 + 6I_3 = 20\\ &-6I_3 - 8I_2 + 5I_1 = 0\\ &12I_5 + 4I_6 + 8I_2 = 40 \end{aligned} \right.

Bu 6 noma’lumli tenglamalar sistemasining yechimi quyida kontur toklar va tugun potensiallar usullari yordamida topiladi.

2. Kontur toklar usuli

Kontur toklarini I₁₁, I₂₂, I₃₃ bilan belgilab, ularni soat mili yo’nalishida yo’naltiramiz (3-rasm).

Kontur toklar usuli tenglamalarining umumiy ko’rinishi:

{I11(R3+R4+R5)−I22R3−I33R5=E3−I11R3+I22(R1+R2+R3)−I33R2=E1−E2−E3−I11R5−I22R2+I33(R2+R5+R6)=E2 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &I_{11}(R_3+R_4+R_5) - I_{22}R_3 - I_{33}R_5 = E_3\\ &-I_{11}R_3 + I_{22}(R_1+R_2+R_3) - I_{33}R_2 = E_1 - E_2 - E_3\\ &-I_{11}R_5 - I_{22}R_2 + I_{33}(R_2+R_5+R_6) = E_2 \end{aligned} \right.

Bu yerda: R₁₁ = R₃ + R₄ + R₅ = 6 + 10 + 12 = 28 Om; R₂₂ = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ = 5 + 8 + 6 = 19 Om; R₃₃ = R₂ + R₅ + R₆ = 8 + 12 + 4 = 24 Om — konturlarning xususiy qarshiliklari; R₁₂ = R₃ = 6 Om, R₁₃ = R₅ = 12 Om, R₂₃ = R₂ = 8 Om — qo’shni konturlarning umumiy qarshiliklari (kontur toklari umumiy tarmoqda qarama-qarshi yo’nalgani uchun tenglamada «−» ishora bilan olinadi). Konturlarning EYuKlari (aylanish yo’nalishiga mos EYuK «+», teskari EYuK «−»): E₁₁ = E₃ = 20 V; E₂₂ = E₁ − E₂ − E₃ = 0 V; E₃₃ = E₂ = 40 V.

Son qiymatlarini qo’yamiz:

{28I11−6I22−12I33=20−6I11+19I22−8I33=0−12I11−8I22+24I33=40 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &28\,I_{11} - 6\,I_{22} - 12\,I_{33} = 20\\ &-6\,I_{11} + 19\,I_{22} - 8\,I_{33} = 0\\ &-12\,I_{11} - 8\,I_{22} + 24\,I_{33} = 40 \end{aligned} \right.

Tenglamalar sistemasini Kramer usuli bilan yechamiz. Bosh determinant:

Δ=∣28−6−12−619−8−12−824∣=6224.\Delta = \begin{vmatrix} 28 & -6 & -12 \\ -6 & 19 & -8 \\ -12 & -8 & 24 \end{vmatrix} = 6224.

Yordamchi determinantlar (tegishli ustun ozod hadlar bilan almashtiriladi):

Δ11=∣20−6−12019−840−824∣=18880.\Delta_{11} = \begin{vmatrix} 20 & -6 & -12 \\ 0 & 19 & -8 \\ 40 & -8 & 24 \end{vmatrix} = 18880.

Δ22=∣2820−12−60−8−124024∣=16640.\Delta_{22} = \begin{vmatrix} 28 & 20 & -12 \\ -6 & 0 & -8 \\ -12 & 40 & 24 \end{vmatrix} = 16640.

Δ33=∣28−620−6190−12−840∣=25360.\Delta_{33} = \begin{vmatrix} 28 & -6 & 20 \\ -6 & 19 & 0 \\ -12 & -8 & 40 \end{vmatrix} = 25360.

Kontur toklari:

I11=Δ11Δ=188806224=3,033A;I22=Δ22Δ=166406224=2,674A;I33=Δ33Δ=253606224=4,075A. \begin{aligned} I_{11} &= \dfrac{\Delta_{11}}{\Delta} = \dfrac{18880}{6224} = 3{,}033\ \text{A};\\ I_{22} &= \dfrac{\Delta_{22}}{\Delta} = \dfrac{16640}{6224} = 2{,}674\ \text{A};\\ I_{33} &= \dfrac{\Delta_{33}}{\Delta} = \dfrac{25360}{6224} = 4{,}075\ \text{A}. \end{aligned}

Kontur toklari orqali tarmoq toklarini aniqlaymiz (tarmoq tokining yo’nalishi bilan mos kontur toki «+», teskari kontur toki «−» bilan olinadi):

I1=I22=2,674A;I2=−I22+I33=−2,674+4,075=1,401A;I3=I11−I22=3,033−2,674=0,360A;I4=I11=3,033A;I5=−I11+I33=−3,033+4,075=1,041A;I6=I33=4,075A. \begin{aligned} I_1 &= I_{22} = 2{,}674\ \text{A};\\ I_2 &= -I_{22} + I_{33} = -2{,}674 + 4{,}075 = 1{,}401\ \text{A};\\ I_3 &= I_{11} - I_{22} = 3{,}033 - 2{,}674 = 0{,}360\ \text{A};\\ I_4 &= I_{11} = 3{,}033\ \text{A};\\ I_5 &= -I_{11} + I_{33} = -3{,}033 + 4{,}075 = 1{,}041\ \text{A};\\ I_6 &= I_{33} = 4{,}075\ \text{A}. \end{aligned}

Barcha tarmoq toklari musbat chiqdi, demak 2-rasmda tanlangan yo’nalishlar toklarning haqiqiy yo’nalishlari bilan mos keladi.

4. Toklarni Kirxgof qonunlari bo’yicha tekshirish

Kirxgofning birinchi qonuni bo’yicha:

bu yerda Uxx=φd−φaU_{xx} = \varphi_d - \varphi_a — 1-tarmoq uzilgandagi (salt ishlash rejimidagi) d va a tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish; RekvR_{ekv} — EYuKlar qisqa tutashtirilgandagi zanjirning d va a qisqichlariga nisbatan kirish qarshiligi.

5.1. Salt ishlash kuchlanishi UxxU_{xx} ni aniqlash

1-tarmoq uzilganda (5-rasm) d va a nuqtalar tugun bo’lmay qoladi va ular orqali o’tgan tarmoqlar ketma-ket ulanadi. Natijada zanjir b va c tugunlar orasida parallel ulangan uchta tarmoqdan iborat bo’ladi:

  • b–d–c tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₂ + R₆ = 8 + 4 = 12 Om, b tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK E₂ = 40 V;
  • b–a–c tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₃ + R₄ = 6 + 10 = 16 Om, b tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK −E₃ = -20 V;
  • b–c tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₅ = 12 Om, EYuK yo’q;

Ikki tugun usuli bo’yicha b va c tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish (φc=0\varphi_c = 0):

Ubc=φb=E2R2+R6+−E3R3+R41R2+R6+1R3+R4+1R5=4012−2016112+116+112=2,08330,2292=9,091V. Salt ishlash rejimidagi tarmoq toklari (b tugundan c tugunga qarab hisoblanadi; manfiy qiymat tok aslida teskari yo’nalganini bildiradi):

d va a nuqtalar potensiallarini b tugundan boshlab, tarmoqlar bo’ylab Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni asosida topamiz (har bir rezistorda tok yo’nalishida potensial I·R ga kamayadi, EYuK yo’nalishida E ga ortadi):

5.2. Ekvivalent qarshilik RekvR_{ekv} ni aniqlash
