Sxemani tahlil qilish va tok yo’nalishlarini tanlash

Sxemada 4 ta tugun (a, b, c, d) va 6 ta tarmoq bor (n = 4, m = 6). Har bir tarmoq raqami undagi rezistor raqamiga teng:

Tarmoq Tarkibi Ulanish tugunlari Tokning tanlangan yo’nalishi
1 R₁, E₁ a – d d → a
2 R₂, E₂ b – c b → c
3 R₃ a – c c → a
4 R₄ c – d c → d
5 R₅ b – d d → b
6 R₆ a – b a → b

Kirxgofning I qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: n − 1 = 4 − 1 = 3. Kirxgofning II qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: m − (n − 1) = 6 − 3 = 3. Jami 6 ta tenglama — 6 ta noma’lum tokni topish uchun yetarli.

Tarmoq toklarining shartli musbat yo’nalishlari 2-rasmda qizil strelkalar bilan ko’rsatilgan. Konturlarni aylanib chiqish yo’nalishi — soat mili bo’yicha (yashil strelkalar).

5. Ekvivalent generator usuli bilan I₂ tokini aniqlash

Ekvivalent generator usuliga ko’ra 2-tarmoqqa nisbatan qolgan barcha zanjir EYuKi Eekv=UxxE_{ekv} = U_{xx} va ichki qarshiligi RekvR_{ekv} bo’lgan bitta ekvivalent manba bilan almashtiriladi (7-rasm). Shunda:

I2=Uxx+E2R2+Rekv,I_2 = \dfrac{U_{xx} + E_2}{R_2 + R_{ekv}},

bu yerda Uxx=φb−φcU_{xx} = \varphi_b - \varphi_c — 2-tarmoq uzilgandagi (salt ishlash rejimidagi) b va c tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish; RekvR_{ekv} — EYuKlar qisqa tutashtirilgandagi zanjirning b va c qisqichlariga nisbatan kirish qarshiligi.

5.1. Salt ishlash kuchlanishi UxxU_{xx} ni aniqlash

2-tarmoq uzilganda (5-rasm) b va c nuqtalar tugun bo’lmay qoladi va ular orqali o’tgan tarmoqlar ketma-ket ulanadi. Natijada zanjir a va d tugunlar orasida parallel ulangan uchta tarmoqdan iborat bo’ladi:

  • a–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₁ = 4 Om, a tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK E₁ = 70 V;
  • a–c–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₃ + R₄ = 8 + 6 = 14 Om, EYuK yo’q;
  • a–b–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₆ + R₅ = 12 + 10 = 22 Om, EYuK yo’q;

Ikki tugun usuli bo’yicha a va d tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish (φd=0\varphi_d = 0):

Uad=φa=E1R11R1+1R3+R4+1R6+R5=70414+114+122=17,50,3669=47,699V. U_{ad} = \varphi_a = \dfrac{\dfrac{E_1}{R_1}}{\dfrac{1}{R_1}+\dfrac{1}{R_3+R_4}+\dfrac{1}{R_6+R_5}} = \dfrac{\dfrac{70}{4}}{\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{14}+\dfrac{1}{22}} = \dfrac{17{,}5}{0{,}3669} = 47{,}699\ \text{V}.

Salt ishlash rejimidagi tarmoq toklari (a tugundan d tugunga qarab hisoblanadi; manfiy qiymat tok aslida teskari yo’nalganini bildiradi):

I1xx=φa−φd−E1R1=47,699−0−704=−5,575A(yo’l a–d;tok d→a);I3xx=I4xx=φa−φdR3+R4=47,699−014=3,407A(yo’l a–c–d;tok a→d);I6xx=I5xx=φa−φdR6+R5=47,699−022=2,168A(yo’l a–b–d;tok a→d). \begin{aligned} I_{1xx} &= \dfrac{\varphi_a - \varphi_d - E_1}{R_1} = \dfrac{47{,}699 - 0 - 70}{4} = -5{,}575\ \text{A} \quad (\text{yo'l } a–d;\ \text{tok } d\to a);\\ I_{3xx} = I_{4xx} &= \dfrac{\varphi_a - \varphi_d}{R_3+R_4} = \dfrac{47{,}699 - 0}{14} = 3{,}407\ \text{A} \quad (\text{yo'l } a–c–d;\ \text{tok } a\to d);\\ I_{6xx} = I_{5xx} &= \dfrac{\varphi_a - \varphi_d}{R_6+R_5} = \dfrac{47{,}699 - 0}{22} = 2{,}168\ \text{A} \quad (\text{yo'l } a–b–d;\ \text{tok } a\to d). \end{aligned}

b va c nuqtalar potensiallarini a tugundan boshlab, tarmoqlar bo’ylab Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni asosida topamiz (har bir rezistorda tok yo’nalishida potensial I·R ga kamayadi, EYuK yo’nalishida E ga ortadi):

Uxx=φb−φc=21,681−20,442=1,239V. U_{xx} = \varphi_b - \varphi_c = 21{,}681 - 20{,}442 = 1{,}239\ \text{V}.

5.2. Ekvivalent qarshilik RekvR_{ekv} ni aniqlash
