Sxemani tahlil qilish va tok yo’nalishlarini tanlash

Sxemada 4 ta tugun (a, b, c, d) va 6 ta tarmoq bor (n = 4, m = 6). Har bir tarmoq raqami undagi rezistor raqamiga teng:

Tarmoq Tarkibi Ulanish tugunlari Tokning tanlangan yo’nalishi
1 R₁, E₁ a – d a → d
2 R₂, E₂ c – d d → c
3 R₃, E₃ a – c c → a
4 R₄ a – b a → b
5 R₅ b – c c → b
6 R₆ b – d b → d

Kirxgofning I qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: n − 1 = 4 − 1 = 3. Kirxgofning II qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: m − (n − 1) = 6 − 3 = 3. Jami 6 ta tenglama — 6 ta noma’lum tokni topish uchun yetarli.

Tarmoq toklarining shartli musbat yo’nalishlari 2-rasmda qizil strelkalar bilan ko’rsatilgan. Konturlarni aylanib chiqish yo’nalishi — soat mili bo’yicha (yashil strelkalar).

1. Kirxgof qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish

5. Ekvivalent generator usuli bilan I₂ tokini aniqlash

Ekvivalent generator usuliga ko’ra 2-tarmoqqa nisbatan qolgan barcha zanjir EYuKi Eekv=UxxE_{ekv} = U_{xx} va ichki qarshiligi RekvR_{ekv} bo’lgan bitta ekvivalent manba bilan almashtiriladi (7-rasm). Shunda:

I2=Uxx+E2R2+Rekv,I_2 = \dfrac{U_{xx} + E_2}{R_2 + R_{ekv}},

bu yerda Uxx=φd−φcU_{xx} = \varphi_d - \varphi_c — 2-tarmoq uzilgandagi (salt ishlash rejimidagi) d va c tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish; RekvR_{ekv} — EYuKlar qisqa tutashtirilgandagi zanjirning d va c qisqichlariga nisbatan kirish qarshiligi.

2-tarmoq uzilganda (5-rasm) d va c nuqtalar tugun bo’lmay qoladi va ular orqali o’tgan tarmoqlar ketma-ket ulanadi. Natijada zanjir a va b tugunlar orasida parallel ulangan uchta tarmoqdan iborat bo’ladi:

  • a–d–b tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₁ + R₆ = 4 + 12 = 16 Om, a tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK E₁ = 70 V;
  • a–c–b tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₃ + R₅ = 8 + 10 = 18 Om, a tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK E₃ = 40 V;
  • a–b tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₄ = 6 Om, EYuK yo’q;

Ikki tugun usuli bo’yicha a va b tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish (φb=0\varphi_b = 0):

Uab=φa=E1R1+R6+E3R3+R51R1+R6+1R3+R5+1R4=7016+4018116+118+16=6,59720,2847=23,171V. 5.2. Ekvivalent qarshilik RekvR_{ekv} ni aniqlash

Barcha EYuK manbalarini ularning ichki qarshiligi (nol) bilan almashtiramiz va 2-tarmoqni uzilgan holda qoldiramiz (6-rasm). Hosil bo’lgan passiv sxemani d va c qisqichlarga nisbatan bosqichma-bosqich soddalashtiramiz.

a, b, c tugunlar orasidagi R4R_4, R5R_5, R3R_3 qarshiliklar uchburchak hosil qiladi. Uni ekvivalent yulduzga almashtiramiz (yulduz markazi — O nuqta):

Ra=R4⋅R3R4+R5+R3=6⋅824=2Om;Rb=R4⋅R5R4+R5+R3=6⋅1024=2,5Om;Rc=R3⋅R5R4+R5+R3=8⋅1024=3,3333Om. \begin{aligned} R_{a} &= \dfrac{R_4\cdot R_3}{R_4 + R_5 + R_3} = \dfrac{6\cdot 8}{24} = 2\ \text{Om};\\ R_{b} &= \dfrac{R_4\cdot R_5}{R_4 + R_5 + R_3} = \dfrac{6\cdot 10}{24} = 2{,}5\ \text{Om};\\ R_{c} &= \dfrac{R_3\cdot R_5}{R_4 + R_5 + R_3} = \dfrac{8\cdot 10}{24} = 3{,}3333\ \text{Om}. \end{aligned}

b nuqta orqali R6R_6 va RbR_{b} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re1=R6+Rb=12+2,5=14,5Om.R_{e1} = R_6 + R_{b} = 12 + 2{,}5 = 14{,}5\ \text{Om}.

a nuqta orqali R1R_1 va RaR_{a} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re2=R1+Ra=4+2=6Om.R_{e2} = R_1 + R_{a} = 4 + 2 = 6\ \text{Om}.

d va O nuqtalar orasidagi Re1R_{e1} va Re2R_{e2} qarshiliklar parallel ulangan:

Re3=Re1⋅Re2Re1+Re2=14,5⋅614,5+6=4,2439Om.R_{e3} = \dfrac{R_{e1}\cdot R_{e2}}{R_{e1} + R_{e2}} = \dfrac{14{,}5\cdot 6}{14{,}5 + 6} = 4{,}2439\ \text{Om}.

O nuqta orqali RcR_{c} va Re3R_{e3} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re4=Rc+Re3=3,3333+4,2439=7,5772Om.R_{e4} = R_{c} + R_{e3} = 3{,}3333 + 4{,}2439 = 7{,}5772\ \text{Om}.

Natijada: Rekv=7,5772R_{ekv} = 7{,}5772 Om.

5.3. I₂ tokini hisoblash

I2=Uxx+E2R2+Rekv=(−25,772)+1105+7,5772=84,22812,5772=6,697A. I_2 = \dfrac{U_{xx} + E_2}{R_2 + R_{ekv}} = \dfrac{(-25{,}772) + 110}{5 + 7{,}5772} = \dfrac{84{,}228}{12{,}5772} = 6{,}697\ \text{A}.

Kontur toklar usulida topilgan qiymat bilan solishtiramiz:

γ=|I2−I2,eg|I2⋅100%=|6,697−6,697|6,697⋅100%=0,00%. \gamma = \dfrac{|I_2 - I_{2,eg}|}{I_2}\cdot 100\% = \dfrac{|6{,}697 - 6{,}697|}{6{,}697}\cdot 100\% = 0{,}00\%.

Ekvivalent generator usuli natijasi kontur toklar va tugun potensiallar usullari natijalari bilan mos keldi.

6. Quvvat muvozanati

Manbalar quvvati: EYuK yo’nalishi tok yo’nalishiga mos kelsa, manba energiya beradi (E·I «+» ishora bilan), teskari bo’lsa — energiya iste’mol qiladi («−» ishora bilan). 1-tarmoqda I₁ toki E₁ ga teskari yo’nalgan, shuning uchun E₁ manbai iste’molchi rejimida ishlaydi.

Pmanba=−E1I1+E2I2+E3I3=−70⋅1,810+110⋅6,697+40⋅4,910=−126,70+736,67+196,40=806,37Vt. P_{manba} = -E_1I_1 + E_2I_2 + E_3I_3 = -70\cdot 1{,}810 + 110\cdot 6{,}697 + 40\cdot 4{,}910 = -126{,}70 + 736{,}67 + 196{,}40 = 806{,}37\ \text{Vt}.

Iste’molchilar (rezistorlar) quvvati:

Pist=I12R1+I22R2+I32R3+I42R4+I52R5+I62R6==1,8102⋅4+6,6972⋅5+4,9102⋅8+3,1002⋅6+1,7872⋅10+4,8872⋅12==13,104+224,249+192,865+57,660+31,934+286,593=806,405Vt. \begin{aligned} P_{ist} &= I_1^2R_1 + I_2^2R_2 + I_3^2R_3 + I_4^2R_4 + I_5^2R_5 + I_6^2R_6 =\\ &= 1{,}810^2\cdot 4 + 6{,}697^2\cdot 5 + 4{,}910^2\cdot 8 + 3{,}100^2\cdot 6 + 1{,}787^2\cdot 10 + 4{,}887^2\cdot 12 =\\ &= 13{,}104 + 224{,}249 + 192{,}865 + 57{,}660 + 31{,}934 + 286{,}593 = 806{,}405\ \text{Vt}. \end{aligned}

Hisoblash xatoligi:

γ=|Pmanba−Pist|Pmanba⋅100%=|806,37−806,405|806,37⋅100%=0,0044%. \gamma = \dfrac{|P_{manba} - P_{ist}|}{P_{manba}}\cdot 100\% = \dfrac{|806{,}37 - 806{,}405|}{806{,}37}\cdot 100\% = 0{,}0044\%.

Quvvat muvozanati bajarildi: Pmanba≈PistP_{manba} \approx P_{ist} (farq faqat toklarni yaxlitlash bilan bog’liq).
