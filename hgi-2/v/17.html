Eslatma: 17-sxemada E₃ manbai yo’q, shuning uchun u hisobda qatnashmaydi.

Topshiriq. Berilgan sxema uchun:

  1. Kirxgofning birinchi va ikkinchi qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish;
  2. kontur toklar usuli bo’yicha tarmoq toklarini topish;
  3. tugun potensiallar usuli bo’yicha tarmoq toklarini topish;
  4. topilgan toklarni Kirxgofning I va II qonunlari asosida tekshirish;
  5. ekvivalent generator usuli yordamida 1-tarmoqdagi tokni (I₁) aniqlash;
  6. quvvat muvozanati tenglamasini tuzish va hisoblash xatoligini aniqlash.

Sxemani tahlil qilish va tok yo’nalishlarini tanlash

Sxemada 4 ta tugun (a, b, c, d) va 6 ta tarmoq bor (n = 4, m = 6). Har bir tarmoq raqami undagi rezistor raqamiga teng:

Tarmoq Tarkibi Ulanish tugunlari Tokning tanlangan yo’nalishi
1 R₁, E₁ a – b a → b
2 R₂, E₂ c – d d → c
3 R₃ b – c b → c
4 R₄ b – d b → d
5 R₅ a – d d → a
6 R₆ a – c c → a

Kirxgofning I qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: n − 1 = 4 − 1 = 3. Kirxgofning II qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: m − (n − 1) = 6 − 3 = 3. Jami 6 ta tenglama — 6 ta noma’lum tokni topish uchun yetarli.

Tarmoq toklarining shartli musbat yo’nalishlari 2-rasmda qizil strelkalar bilan ko’rsatilgan. Konturlarni aylanib chiqish yo’nalishi — soat mili bo’yicha (yashil strelkalar).

1. Kirxgof qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish

Kirxgofning birinchi qonuni (tugunga kiruvchi toklar «+», chiquvchi toklar «−» ishora bilan olinadi):

Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni (tok yoki EYuK yo’nalishi kontur aylanish yo’nalishiga mos kelsa «+», teskari bo’lsa «−»):

{−I1+I5+I6=0I1−I3−I4=0I2+I3−I6=0−6I6−3I3−2I1=−11015I4+5I5+2I1=110−15I4+3I3−4I2=−60 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &-I_1 + I_5 + I_6 = 0\\ &I_1 - I_3 - I_4 = 0\\ &I_2 + I_3 - I_6 = 0\\ &-6I_6 - 3I_3 - 2I_1 = -110\\ &15I_4 + 5I_5 + 2I_1 = 110\\ &-15I_4 + 3I_3 - 4I_2 = -60 \end{aligned} \right.

Bu 6 noma’lumli tenglamalar sistemasining yechimi quyida kontur toklar va tugun potensiallar usullari yordamida topiladi.

2. Kontur toklar usuli

Kontur toklarini I₁₁, I₂₂, I₃₃ bilan belgilab, ularni soat mili yo’nalishida yo’naltiramiz (3-rasm).

Kontur toklar usuli tenglamalarining umumiy ko’rinishi:

{I11(R1+R3+R6)−I22R1−I33R3=−E1−I11R1+I22(R1+R4+R5)−I33R4=E1−I11R3−I22R4+I33(R2+R3+R4)=−E2 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &I_{11}(R_1+R_3+R_6) - I_{22}R_1 - I_{33}R_3 = -E_1\\ &-I_{11}R_1 + I_{22}(R_1+R_4+R_5) - I_{33}R_4 = E_1\\ &-I_{11}R_3 - I_{22}R_4 + I_{33}(R_2+R_3+R_4) = -E_2 \end{aligned} \right.

Bu yerda: R₁₁ = R₁ + R₃ + R₆ = 2 + 3 + 6 = 11 Om; R₂₂ = R₁ + R₄ + R₅ = 2 + 15 + 5 = 22 Om; R₃₃ = R₂ + R₃ + R₄ = 4 + 3 + 15 = 22 Om — konturlarning xususiy qarshiliklari; R₁₂ = R₁ = 2 Om, R₁₃ = R₃ = 3 Om, R₂₃ = R₄ = 15 Om — qo’shni konturlarning umumiy qarshiliklari (kontur toklari umumiy tarmoqda qarama-qarshi yo’nalgani uchun tenglamada «−» ishora bilan olinadi). Konturlarning EYuKlari (aylanish yo’nalishiga mos EYuK «+», teskari EYuK «−»): E₁₁ = −E₁ = -110 V; E₂₂ = E₁ = 110 V; E₃₃ = −E₂ = -60 V.

Son qiymatlarini qo’yamiz:

{11I11−2I22−3I33=−110−2I11+22I22−15I33=110−3I11−15I22+22I33=−60 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &11\,I_{11} - 2\,I_{22} - 3\,I_{33} = -110\\ &-2\,I_{11} + 22\,I_{22} - 15\,I_{33} = 110\\ &-3\,I_{11} - 15\,I_{22} + 22\,I_{33} = -60 \end{aligned} \right.

Δ=∣11−2−3−222−15−3−1522∣=2383.\Delta = \begin{vmatrix} 11 & -2 & -3 \\ -2 & 22 & -15 \\ -3 & -15 & 22 \end{vmatrix} = 2383.

Kontur toklari orqali tarmoq toklarini aniqlaymiz (tarmoq tokining yo’nalishi bilan mos kontur toki «+», teskari kontur toki «−» bilan olinadi):

Barcha tarmoq toklari musbat chiqdi, demak 2-rasmda tanlangan yo’nalishlar toklarning haqiqiy yo’nalishlari bilan mos keladi.

3. Tugun potensiallar usuli

d tugun potensialini nolga tenglaymiz: φd=0\varphi_d = 0 (4-rasm). Qolgan a, b, c tugunlar uchun Kirxgofning birinchi qonuni asosida tugun potensiallar tenglamalarini tuzamiz. {φa(1R1+1R5+1R6)−φb1R1−φc1R6=−E1R1φb(1R1+1R3+1R4)−φa1R1−φc1R3=E1R1φc(1R2+1R3+1R6)−φa1R6−φb1R3=E2R2 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &\varphi_a\left(\dfrac{1}{R_1}+\dfrac{1}{R_5}+\dfrac{1}{R_6}\right) - \varphi_b\dfrac{1}{R_1} - \varphi_c\dfrac{1}{R_6} = -\dfrac{E_1}{R_1}\\ &\varphi_b\left(\dfrac{1}{R_1}+\dfrac{1}{R_3}+\dfrac{1}{R_4}\right) - \varphi_a\dfrac{1}{R_1} - \varphi_c\dfrac{1}{R_3} = \dfrac{E_1}{R_1}\\ &\varphi_c\left(\dfrac{1}{R_2}+\dfrac{1}{R_3}+\dfrac{1}{R_6}\right) - \varphi_a\dfrac{1}{R_6} - \varphi_b\dfrac{1}{R_3} = \dfrac{E_2}{R_2} \end{aligned} \right.

Tugun potensiallar usuli bilan topilgan toklar kontur toklar usuli natijalari bilan to’liq mos keldi.

4. Toklarni Kirxgof qonunlari bo’yicha tekshirish

Kirxgofning birinchi qonuni bo’yicha:

«a» tugun:−I1+I5+I6=−12,606+2,342+10,264=0,000;«b» tugun:I1−I3−I4=12,606−7,734−4,872=0,000;«c» tugun:I2+I3−I6=2,530+7,734−10,264=0,000;«d» tugun:−I2+I4−I5=−2,530+4,872−2,342=0,000. \begin{aligned} &\text{«a» tugun:} && -I_1 + I_5 + I_6 = -12{,}606 + 2{,}342 + 10{,}264 = 0{,}000;\\ &\text{«b» tugun:} && I_1 - I_3 - I_4 = 12{,}606 - 7{,}734 - 4{,}872 = 0{,}000;\\ &\text{«c» tugun:} && I_2 + I_3 - I_6 = 2{,}530 + 7{,}734 - 10{,}264 = 0{,}000;\\ &\text{«d» tugun:} && -I_2 + I_4 - I_5 = -2{,}530 + 4{,}872 - 2{,}342 = 0{,}000. \end{aligned}

Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni bo’yicha:

Barcha tenglamalar bajarildi (farq faqat toklarni uch xonagacha yaxlitlash hisobiga), demak toklar to’g’ri topilgan.

5. Ekvivalent generator usuli bilan I₁ tokini aniqlash

Ekvivalent generator usuliga ko’ra 1-tarmoqqa nisbatan qolgan barcha zanjir EYuKi Eekv=UxxE_{ekv} = U_{xx} va ichki qarshiligi RekvR_{ekv} bo’lgan bitta ekvivalent manba bilan almashtiriladi (7-rasm). Shunda:

I1=Uxx+E1R1+Rekv,I_1 = \dfrac{U_{xx} + E_1}{R_1 + R_{ekv}},

bu yerda Uxx=φa−φbU_{xx} = \varphi_a - \varphi_b — 1-tarmoq uzilgandagi (salt ishlash rejimidagi) a va b tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish; RekvR_{ekv} — EYuKlar qisqa tutashtirilgandagi zanjirning a va b qisqichlariga nisbatan kirish qarshiligi.

5.1. Salt ishlash kuchlanishi UxxU_{xx} ni aniqlash

1-tarmoq uzilganda (5-rasm) a va b nuqtalar tugun bo’lmay qoladi va ular orqali o’tgan tarmoqlar ketma-ket ulanadi. Natijada zanjir c va d tugunlar orasida parallel ulangan uchta tarmoqdan iborat bo’ladi:

  • c–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₂ = 4 Om, c tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK E₂ = 60 V;
  • c–b–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₃ + R₄ = 3 + 15 = 18 Om, EYuK yo’q;
  • c–a–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₆ + R₅ = 6 + 5 = 11 Om, EYuK yo’q;

Ikki tugun usuli bo’yicha c va d tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish (φd=0\varphi_d = 0):

Ucd=φc=E2R21R2+1R3+R4+1R6+R5=60414+118+111=150,3965=37,834V. U_{cd} = \varphi_c = \dfrac{\dfrac{E_2}{R_2}}{\dfrac{1}{R_2}+\dfrac{1}{R_3+R_4}+\dfrac{1}{R_6+R_5}} = \dfrac{\dfrac{60}{4}}{\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{18}+\dfrac{1}{11}} = \dfrac{15}{0{,}3965} = 37{,}834\ \text{V}.

Salt ishlash rejimidagi tarmoq toklari (c tugundan d tugunga qarab hisoblanadi; manfiy qiymat tok aslida teskari yo’nalganini bildiradi):

I2xx=φc−φd−E2R2=37,834−0−604=−5,541A(yo’l c–d;tok d→c);I3xx=I4xx=φc−φdR3+R4=37,834−018=2,102A(yo’l c–b–d;tok c→d);I6xx=I5xx=φc−φdR6+R5=37,834−011=3,439A(yo’l c–a–d;tok c→d). \begin{aligned} I_{2xx} &= \dfrac{\varphi_c - \varphi_d - E_2}{R_2} = \dfrac{37{,}834 - 0 - 60}{4} = -5{,}541\ \text{A} \quad (\text{yo'l } c–d;\ \text{tok } d\to c);\\ I_{3xx} = I_{4xx} &= \dfrac{\varphi_c - \varphi_d}{R_3+R_4} = \dfrac{37{,}834 - 0}{18} = 2{,}102\ \text{A} \quad (\text{yo'l } c–b–d;\ \text{tok } c\to d);\\ I_{6xx} = I_{5xx} &= \dfrac{\varphi_c - \varphi_d}{R_6+R_5} = \dfrac{37{,}834 - 0}{11} = 3{,}439\ \text{A} \quad (\text{yo'l } c–a–d;\ \text{tok } c\to d). \end{aligned}

Tekshirish («c» tugun, chiquvchi toklar yig’indisi): (-5,541) + 2,102 + 3,439 = 0,000 ≈ 0.

a va b nuqtalar potensiallarini c tugundan boshlab, tarmoqlar bo’ylab Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni asosida topamiz (har bir rezistorda tok yo’nalishida potensial I·R ga kamayadi, EYuK yo’nalishida E ga ortadi):

φa=φc−I6xxR6=37,834−3,439⋅6=17,197V;φb=φc−I3xxR3=37,834−2,102⋅3=31,529V. \begin{aligned} \varphi_a &= \varphi_c - I_{6xx}R_6 = 37{,}834 - 3{,}439\cdot 6 = 17{,}197\ \text{V};\\ \varphi_b &= \varphi_c - I_{3xx}R_3 = 37{,}834 - 2{,}102\cdot 3 = 31{,}529\ \text{V}. \end{aligned}

Uxx=φa−φb=17,197−31,529=−14,331V. U_{xx} = \varphi_a - \varphi_b = 17{,}197 - 31{,}529 = -14{,}331\ \text{V}.

5.2. Ekvivalent qarshilik RekvR_{ekv} ni aniqlash
