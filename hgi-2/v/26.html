Topshiriq. Berilgan sxema uchun:

  1. Kirxgofning birinchi va ikkinchi qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish;
  2. kontur toklar usuli bo’yicha tarmoq toklarini topish;
  3. tugun potensiallar usuli bo’yicha tarmoq toklarini topish;
  4. topilgan toklarni Kirxgofning I va II qonunlari asosida tekshirish;
  5. ekvivalent generator usuli yordamida 2-tarmoqdagi tokni (I₂) aniqlash;
  6. quvvat muvozanati tenglamasini tuzish va hisoblash xatoligini aniqlash.

Sxemani tahlil qilish va tok yo’nalishlarini tanlash

Sxemada 4 ta tugun (a, b, c, d) va 6 ta tarmoq bor (n = 4, m = 6). Har bir tarmoq raqami undagi rezistor raqamiga teng:

Tarmoq Tarkibi Ulanish tugunlari Tokning tanlangan yo’nalishi
1 R₁, E₁ a – c a → c
2 R₂, E₂ a – b b → a
3 R₃, E₃ b – d d → b
4 R₄ c – d c → d
5 R₅ b – c b → c
6 R₆ a – d a → d

Kirxgofning I qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: n − 1 = 4 − 1 = 3. Kirxgofning II qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: m − (n − 1) = 6 − 3 = 3. Jami 6 ta tenglama — 6 ta noma’lum tokni topish uchun yetarli.

Tarmoq toklarining shartli musbat yo’nalishlari 2-rasmda qizil strelkalar bilan ko’rsatilgan. Konturlarni aylanib chiqish yo’nalishi — soat mili bo’yicha (yashil strelkalar).

bu yerda Uxx=φb−φaU_{xx} = \varphi_b - \varphi_a — 2-tarmoq uzilgandagi (salt ishlash rejimidagi) b va a tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish; RekvR_{ekv} — EYuKlar qisqa tutashtirilgandagi zanjirning b va a qisqichlariga nisbatan kirish qarshiligi.

  • c–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₄ = 8 Om, EYuK yo’q;
  • c–b–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₅ + R₃ = 10 + 4 = 14 Om, c tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK E₃ = 30 V;

Ikki tugun usuli bo’yicha c va d tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish (φd=0\varphi_d = 0):

Ucd=φc=−E1R1+R6+E3R5+R31R1+R6+1R4+1R5+R3=−4016+3014116+18+114=−0,35710,2589=−1,379V. 5.2. Ekvivalent qarshilik RekvR_{ekv} ni aniqlash

Barcha EYuK manbalarini ularning ichki qarshiligi (nol) bilan almashtiramiz va 2-tarmoqni uzilgan holda qoldiramiz (6-rasm). Hosil bo’lgan passiv sxemani b va a qisqichlarga nisbatan bosqichma-bosqich soddalashtiramiz.

a, c, d tugunlar orasidagi R1R_1, R4R_4, R6R_6 qarshiliklar uchburchak hosil qiladi. Uni ekvivalent yulduzga almashtiramiz (yulduz markazi — O nuqta):

Ra=R1⋅R6R1+R4+R6=9⋅724=2,625Om;Rc=R1⋅R4R1+R4+R6=9⋅824=3Om;Rd=R6⋅R4R1+R4+R6=7⋅824=2,3333Om. \begin{aligned} R_{a} &= \dfrac{R_1\cdot R_6}{R_1 + R_4 + R_6} = \dfrac{9\cdot 7}{24} = 2{,}625\ \text{Om};\\ R_{c} &= \dfrac{R_1\cdot R_4}{R_1 + R_4 + R_6} = \dfrac{9\cdot 8}{24} = 3\ \text{Om};\\ R_{d} &= \dfrac{R_6\cdot R_4}{R_1 + R_4 + R_6} = \dfrac{7\cdot 8}{24} = 2{,}3333\ \text{Om}. \end{aligned}

c nuqta orqali R5R_5 va RcR_{c} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re1=R5+Rc=10+3=13Om.R_{e1} = R_5 + R_{c} = 10 + 3 = 13\ \text{Om}.

d nuqta orqali R3R_3 va RdR_{d} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re2=R3+Rd=4+2,3333=6,3333Om.R_{e2} = R_3 + R_{d} = 4 + 2{,}3333 = 6{,}3333\ \text{Om}.

b va O nuqtalar orasidagi Re1R_{e1} va Re2R_{e2} qarshiliklar parallel ulangan:

Re3=Re1⋅Re2Re1+Re2=13⋅6,333313+6,3333=4,2586Om.R_{e3} = \dfrac{R_{e1}\cdot R_{e2}}{R_{e1} + R_{e2}} = \dfrac{13\cdot 6{,}3333}{13 + 6{,}3333} = 4{,}2586\ \text{Om}.

O nuqta orqali RaR_{a} va Re3R_{e3} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re4=Ra+Re3=2,625+4,2586=6,8836Om.R_{e4} = R_{a} + R_{e3} = 2{,}625 + 4{,}2586 = 6{,}8836\ \text{Om}.

Natijada: Rekv=6,8836R_{ekv} = 6{,}8836 Om.

5.3. I₂ tokini hisoblash

I2=Uxx+E2R2+Rekv=4,138+12012+6,8836=124,13818,8836=6,574A. I_2 = \dfrac{U_{xx} + E_2}{R_2 + R_{ekv}} = \dfrac{4{,}138 + 120}{12 + 6{,}8836} = \dfrac{124{,}138}{18{,}8836} = 6{,}574\ \text{A}.

Kontur toklar usulida topilgan qiymat bilan solishtiramiz:

γ=|I2−I2,eg|I2⋅100%=|6,574−6,574|6,574⋅100%=0,00%. \gamma = \dfrac{|I_2 - I_{2,eg}|}{I_2}\cdot 100\% = \dfrac{|6{,}574 - 6{,}574|}{6{,}574}\cdot 100\% = 0{,}00\%.

Ekvivalent generator usuli natijasi kontur toklar va tugun potensiallar usullari natijalari bilan mos keldi.

6. Quvvat muvozanati

Manbalar quvvati: EYuK yo’nalishi tok yo’nalishiga mos kelsa, manba energiya beradi (E·I «+» ishora bilan), teskari bo’lsa — energiya iste’mol qiladi («−» ishora bilan). 1-tarmoqda I₁ toki E₁ ga teskari yo’nalgan, shuning uchun E₁ manbai iste’molchi rejimida ishlaydi.

Pmanba=−E1I1+E2I2+E3I3=−40⋅0,221+120⋅6,574+30⋅6,662=−8,84+788,88+199,86=979,90Vt. P_{manba} = -E_1I_1 + E_2I_2 + E_3I_3 = -40\cdot 0{,}221 + 120\cdot 6{,}574 + 30\cdot 6{,}662 = -8{,}84 + 788{,}88 + 199{,}86 = 979{,}90\ \text{Vt}.

Iste’molchilar (rezistorlar) quvvati:

Pist=I12R1+I22R2+I32R3+I42R4+I52R5+I62R6==0,2212⋅9+6,5742⋅12+6,6622⋅4+0,3092⋅8+0,0882⋅10+6,3522⋅7==0,440+518,610+177,529+0,764+0,077+282,435=979,855Vt. \begin{aligned} P_{ist} &= I_1^2R_1 + I_2^2R_2 + I_3^2R_3 + I_4^2R_4 + I_5^2R_5 + I_6^2R_6 =\\ &= 0{,}221^2\cdot 9 + 6{,}574^2\cdot 12 + 6{,}662^2\cdot 4 + 0{,}309^2\cdot 8 + 0{,}088^2\cdot 10 + 6{,}352^2\cdot 7 =\\ &= 0{,}440 + 518{,}610 + 177{,}529 + 0{,}764 + 0{,}077 + 282{,}435 = 979{,}855\ \text{Vt}. \end{aligned}

Hisoblash xatoligi:

γ=|Pmanba−Pist|Pmanba⋅100%=|979,90−979,855|979,90⋅100%=0,0046%. \gamma = \dfrac{|P_{manba} - P_{ist}|}{P_{manba}}\cdot 100\% = \dfrac{|979{,}90 - 979{,}855|}{979{,}90}\cdot 100\% = 0{,}0046\%.
