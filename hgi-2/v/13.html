Eslatma: 13-sxemada E₁ manbai yo’q, shuning uchun u hisobda qatnashmaydi.

Topshiriq. Berilgan sxema uchun:

  1. Kirxgofning birinchi va ikkinchi qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish;
  2. kontur toklar usuli bo’yicha tarmoq toklarini topish;
  3. tugun potensiallar usuli bo’yicha tarmoq toklarini topish;
  4. topilgan toklarni Kirxgofning I va II qonunlari asosida tekshirish;
  5. ekvivalent generator usuli yordamida 1-tarmoqdagi tokni (I₁) aniqlash;
  6. quvvat muvozanati tenglamasini tuzish va hisoblash xatoligini aniqlash.

Sxemani tahlil qilish va tok yo’nalishlarini tanlash

Sxemada 4 ta tugun (a, b, c, d) va 6 ta tarmoq bor (n = 4, m = 6). Har bir tarmoq raqami undagi rezistor raqamiga teng:

Tarmoq Tarkibi Ulanish tugunlari Tokning tanlangan yo’nalishi
1 R₁ a – c a → c
2 R₂, E₂ c – d c → d
3 R₃, E₃ b – c b → c
4 R₄ a – b a → b
5 R₅ b – d d → b
6 R₆ a – d d → a

Kirxgofning I qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: n − 1 = 4 − 1 = 3. Kirxgofning II qonuni bo’yicha mustaqil tenglamalar soni: m − (n − 1) = 6 − 3 = 3. Jami 6 ta tenglama — 6 ta noma’lum tokni topish uchun yetarli.

Tarmoq toklarining shartli musbat yo’nalishlari 2-rasmda qizil strelkalar bilan ko’rsatilgan. Konturlarni aylanib chiqish yo’nalishi — soat mili bo’yicha (yashil strelkalar).

1. Kirxgof qonunlari bo’yicha tenglamalar tuzish

Kirxgofning birinchi qonuni (tugunga kiruvchi toklar «+», chiquvchi toklar «−» ishora bilan olinadi):

«a» tugun uchun:−I1−I4+I6=0;«b» tugun uchun:−I3+I4+I5=0;«c» tugun uchun:I1−I2+I3=0;«d» tugun uchun:I2−I5−I6=0.(tekshirish uchun) \begin{aligned} &\text{«a» tugun uchun:} && -I_1 - I_4 + I_6 = 0;\\ &\text{«b» tugun uchun:} && -I_3 + I_4 + I_5 = 0;\\ &\text{«c» tugun uchun:} && I_1 - I_2 + I_3 = 0;\\ &\text{«d» tugun uchun:} && I_2 - I_5 - I_6 = 0. \quad (\text{tekshirish uchun}) \end{aligned}

Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni (tok yoki EYuK yo’nalishi kontur aylanish yo’nalishiga mos kelsa «+», teskari bo’lsa «−»):

I kontur (c–b–a–c):−I3R3−I4R4+I1R1=−E3;II kontur (a–d–c–a):−I6R6−I2R2−I1R1=−E2;III kontur (d–b–c–d):I5R5+I3R3+I2R2=E3+E2. \begin{aligned} &\text{I kontur (c–b–a–c):} && -I_3R_3 - I_4R_4 + I_1R_1 = -E_3;\\ &\text{II kontur (a–d–c–a):} && -I_6R_6 - I_2R_2 - I_1R_1 = -E_2;\\ &\text{III kontur (d–b–c–d):} && I_5R_5 + I_3R_3 + I_2R_2 = E_3 + E_2. \end{aligned}

Son qiymatlarini qo’ysak:

{−I1−I4+I6=0−I3+I4+I5=0I1−I2+I3=0−8I3−10I4+12I1=−80−9I6−4I2−12I1=−1107I5+8I3+4I2=190 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &-I_1 - I_4 + I_6 = 0\\ &-I_3 + I_4 + I_5 = 0\\ &I_1 - I_2 + I_3 = 0\\ &-8I_3 - 10I_4 + 12I_1 = -80\\ &-9I_6 - 4I_2 - 12I_1 = -110\\ &7I_5 + 8I_3 + 4I_2 = 190 \end{aligned} \right.

Bu 6 noma’lumli tenglamalar sistemasining yechimi quyida kontur toklar va tugun potensiallar usullari yordamida topiladi.

2. Kontur toklar usuli

Kontur toklarini I₁₁, I₂₂, I₃₃ bilan belgilab, ularni soat mili yo’nalishida yo’naltiramiz (3-rasm).

Kontur toklar usuli tenglamalarining umumiy ko’rinishi:

{I11(R1+R3+R4)−I22R1−I33R3=−E3−I11R1+I22(R1+R2+R6)−I33R2=−E2−I11R3−I22R2+I33(R2+R3+R5)=E2+E3 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &I_{11}(R_1+R_3+R_4) - I_{22}R_1 - I_{33}R_3 = -E_3\\ &-I_{11}R_1 + I_{22}(R_1+R_2+R_6) - I_{33}R_2 = -E_2\\ &-I_{11}R_3 - I_{22}R_2 + I_{33}(R_2+R_3+R_5) = E_2 + E_3 \end{aligned} \right.

Bu yerda: R₁₁ = R₁ + R₃ + R₄ = 12 + 8 + 10 = 30 Om; R₂₂ = R₁ + R₂ + R₆ = 12 + 4 + 9 = 25 Om; R₃₃ = R₂ + R₃ + R₅ = 4 + 8 + 7 = 19 Om — konturlarning xususiy qarshiliklari; R₁₂ = R₁ = 12 Om, R₁₃ = R₃ = 8 Om, R₂₃ = R₂ = 4 Om — qo’shni konturlarning umumiy qarshiliklari (kontur toklari umumiy tarmoqda qarama-qarshi yo’nalgani uchun tenglamada «−» ishora bilan olinadi). Konturlarning EYuKlari (aylanish yo’nalishiga mos EYuK «+», teskari EYuK «−»): E₁₁ = −E₃ = -80 V; E₂₂ = −E₂ = -110 V; E₃₃ = E₂ + E₃ = 190 V.

Son qiymatlarini qo’yamiz:

{30I11−12I22−8I33=−80−12I11+25I22−4I33=−110−8I11−4I22+19I33=190 \left\{ \begin{aligned} &30\,I_{11} - 12\,I_{22} - 8\,I_{33} = -80\\ &-12\,I_{11} + 25\,I_{22} - 4\,I_{33} = -110\\ &-8\,I_{11} - 4\,I_{22} + 19\,I_{33} = 190 \end{aligned} \right.

Δ=∣30−12−8−1225−4−8−419∣=8666.\Delta = \begin{vmatrix} 30 & -12 & -8 \\ -12 & 25 & -4 \\ -8 & -4 & 19 \end{vmatrix} = 8666.

Δ11=∣−80−12−8−11025−4190−419∣=−18200.\Delta_{11} = \begin{vmatrix} -80 & -12 & -8 \\ -110 & 25 & -4 \\ 190 & -4 & 19 \end{vmatrix} = -18200.

I₁₁, I₂₂ larning manfiy chiqishi bu kontur toklarining haqiqiy yo’nalishi soat miliga teskari ekanini bildiradi.

Kontur toklari orqali tarmoq toklarini aniqlaymiz (tarmoq tokining yo’nalishi bilan mos kontur toki «+», teskari kontur toki «−» bilan olinadi):

I1=I11−I22=(−2,100)−(−4,087)=1,987A;I2=−I22+I33=−(−4,087)+8,255=12,342A;I3=−I11+I33=−(−2,100)+8,255=10,355A;I4=−I11=−(−2,100)=2,100A;I5=I33=8,255A;I6=−I22=−(−4,087)=4,087A. \begin{aligned} I_1 &= I_{11} - I_{22} = (-2{,}100) - (-4{,}087) = 1{,}987\ \text{A};\\ I_2 &= -I_{22} + I_{33} = -(-4{,}087) + 8{,}255 = 12{,}342\ \text{A};\\ I_3 &= -I_{11} + I_{33} = -(-2{,}100) + 8{,}255 = 10{,}355\ \text{A};\\ I_4 &= -I_{11} = -(-2{,}100) = 2{,}100\ \text{A};\\ I_5 &= I_{33} = 8{,}255\ \text{A};\\ I_6 &= -I_{22} = -(-4{,}087) = 4{,}087\ \text{A}. \end{aligned}

Barcha tarmoq toklari musbat chiqdi, demak 2-rasmda tanlangan yo’nalishlar toklarning haqiqiy yo’nalishlari bilan mos keladi.

4. Toklarni Kirxgof qonunlari bo’yicha tekshirish

Kirxgofning birinchi qonuni bo’yicha:

Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni bo’yicha:

Barcha tenglamalar bajarildi (farq faqat toklarni uch xonagacha yaxlitlash hisobiga), demak toklar to’g’ri topilgan.

5. Ekvivalent generator usuli bilan I₁ tokini aniqlash

Ekvivalent generator usuliga ko’ra 1-tarmoqqa nisbatan qolgan barcha zanjir EYuKi Eekv=UxxE_{ekv} = U_{xx} va ichki qarshiligi RekvR_{ekv} bo’lgan bitta ekvivalent manba bilan almashtiriladi (7-rasm). Shunda:

I1=UxxR1+Rekv,I_1 = \dfrac{U_{xx}}{R_1 + R_{ekv}},

bu yerda Uxx=φa−φcU_{xx} = \varphi_a - \varphi_c — 1-tarmoq uzilgandagi (salt ishlash rejimidagi) a va c tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish; RekvR_{ekv} — EYuKlar qisqa tutashtirilgandagi zanjirning a va c qisqichlariga nisbatan kirish qarshiligi.

5.1. Salt ishlash kuchlanishi UxxU_{xx} ni aniqlash

1-tarmoq uzilganda (5-rasm) a va c nuqtalar tugun bo’lmay qoladi va ular orqali o’tgan tarmoqlar ketma-ket ulanadi. Natijada zanjir b va d tugunlar orasida parallel ulangan uchta tarmoqdan iborat bo’ladi:

  • b–c–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₃ + R₂ = 8 + 4 = 12 Om, b tomonga yo’nalgan EYuK −E₃ − E₂ = -190 V;
  • b–a–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₄ + R₆ = 10 + 9 = 19 Om, EYuK yo’q;
  • b–d tarmog’i: qarshiligi R₅ = 7 Om, EYuK yo’q;

Ikki tugun usuli bo’yicha b va d tugunlar orasidagi kuchlanish (φd=0\varphi_d = 0):

Ubd=φb=−E3−E2R3+R21R3+R2+1R4+R6+1R5=−19012112+119+17=−15,83330,2788=−56,787V. U_{bd} = \varphi_b = \dfrac{\dfrac{-E_3 - E_2}{R_3+R_2}}{\dfrac{1}{R_3+R_2}+\dfrac{1}{R_4+R_6}+\dfrac{1}{R_5}} = \dfrac{-\dfrac{190}{12}}{\dfrac{1}{12}+\dfrac{1}{19}+\dfrac{1}{7}} = \dfrac{-15{,}8333}{0{,}2788} = -56{,}787\ \text{V}.

Salt ishlash rejimidagi tarmoq toklari (b tugundan d tugunga qarab hisoblanadi; manfiy qiymat tok aslida teskari yo’nalganini bildiradi):

I3xx=I2xx=φb−φd+E3+E2R3+R2=(−56,787)−0+19012=11,101A(yo’l b–c–d;tok b→d);I4xx=I6xx=φb−φdR4+R6=(−56,787)−019=−2,989A(yo’l b–a–d;tok d→b);I5xx=φb−φdR5=(−56,787)−07=−8,112A(yo’l b–d;tok d→b). \begin{aligned} I_{3xx} = I_{2xx} &= \dfrac{\varphi_b - \varphi_d + E_3 + E_2}{R_3+R_2} = \dfrac{(-56{,}787) - 0 + 190}{12} = 11{,}101\ \text{A} \quad (\text{yo'l } b–c–d;\ \text{tok } b\to d);\\ I_{4xx} = I_{6xx} &= \dfrac{\varphi_b - \varphi_d}{R_4+R_6} = \dfrac{(-56{,}787) - 0}{19} = -2{,}989\ \text{A} \quad (\text{yo'l } b–a–d;\ \text{tok } d\to b);\\ I_{5xx} &= \dfrac{\varphi_b - \varphi_d}{R_5} = \dfrac{(-56{,}787) - 0}{7} = -8{,}112\ \text{A} \quad (\text{yo'l } b–d;\ \text{tok } d\to b). \end{aligned}

Tekshirish («b» tugun, chiquvchi toklar yig’indisi): 11,101 + (-2,989) + (-8,112) = 0,000 ≈ 0.

a va c nuqtalar potensiallarini b tugundan boshlab, tarmoqlar bo’ylab Kirxgofning ikkinchi qonuni asosida topamiz (har bir rezistorda tok yo’nalishida potensial I·R ga kamayadi, EYuK yo’nalishida E ga ortadi):

φa=φb−I4xxR4=(−56,787)−(−2,989)⋅10=−26,899V;φc=φb−I3xxR3+E3=(−56,787)−11,101⋅8+80=−65,596V. \begin{aligned} \varphi_a &= \varphi_b - I_{4xx}R_4 = (-56{,}787) - (-2{,}989)\cdot 10 = -26{,}899\ \text{V};\\ \varphi_c &= \varphi_b - I_{3xx}R_3 + E_3 = (-56{,}787) - 11{,}101\cdot 8 + 80 = -65{,}596\ \text{V}. \end{aligned}

Uxx=φa−φc=(−26,899)−(−65,596)=38,697V. U_{xx} = \varphi_a - \varphi_c = (-26{,}899) - (-65{,}596) = 38{,}697\ \text{V}.

5.2. Ekvivalent qarshilik RekvR_{ekv} ni aniqlash

Barcha EYuK manbalarini ularning ichki qarshiligi (nol) bilan almashtiramiz va 1-tarmoqni uzilgan holda qoldiramiz (6-rasm). Hosil bo’lgan passiv sxemani a va c qisqichlarga nisbatan bosqichma-bosqich soddalashtiramiz.

a, b, d tugunlar orasidagi R4R_4, R5R_5, R6R_6 qarshiliklar uchburchak hosil qiladi. Uni ekvivalent yulduzga almashtiramiz (yulduz markazi — O nuqta):

Ra=R4⋅R6R4+R5+R6=10⋅926=3,4615Om;Rb=R4⋅R5R4+R5+R6=10⋅726=2,6923Om;Rd=R6⋅R5R4+R5+R6=9⋅726=2,4231Om. \begin{aligned} R_{a} &= \dfrac{R_4\cdot R_6}{R_4 + R_5 + R_6} = \dfrac{10\cdot 9}{26} = 3{,}4615\ \text{Om};\\ R_{b} &= \dfrac{R_4\cdot R_5}{R_4 + R_5 + R_6} = \dfrac{10\cdot 7}{26} = 2{,}6923\ \text{Om};\\ R_{d} &= \dfrac{R_6\cdot R_5}{R_4 + R_5 + R_6} = \dfrac{9\cdot 7}{26} = 2{,}4231\ \text{Om}. \end{aligned}

b nuqta orqali R3R_3 va RbR_{b} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re1=R3+Rb=8+2,6923=10,6923Om.R_{e1} = R_3 + R_{b} = 8 + 2{,}6923 = 10{,}6923\ \text{Om}.

d nuqta orqali R2R_2 va RdR_{d} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re2=R2+Rd=4+2,4231=6,4231Om.R_{e2} = R_2 + R_{d} = 4 + 2{,}4231 = 6{,}4231\ \text{Om}.

c va O nuqtalar orasidagi Re1R_{e1} va Re2R_{e2} qarshiliklar parallel ulangan:

Re3=Re1⋅Re2Re1+Re2=10,6923⋅6,423110,6923+6,4231=4,0126Om.R_{e3} = \dfrac{R_{e1}\cdot R_{e2}}{R_{e1} + R_{e2}} = \dfrac{10{,}6923\cdot 6{,}4231}{10{,}6923 + 6{,}4231} = 4{,}0126\ \text{Om}.

O nuqta orqali RaR_{a} va Re3R_{e3} qarshiliklar ketma-ket ulangan:

Re4=Ra+Re3=3,4615+4,0126=7,4742Om.R_{e4} = R_{a} + R_{e3} = 3{,}4615 + 4{,}0126 = 7{,}4742\ \text{Om}.

5.3. I₁ tokini hisoblash

I1=UxxR1+Rekv=38,69712+7,4742=38,69719,4742=1,987A. I_1 = \dfrac{U_{xx}}{R_1 + R_{ekv}} = \dfrac{38{,}697}{12 + 7{,}4742} = \dfrac{38{,}697}{19{,}4742} = 1{,}987\ \text{A}.

Kontur toklar usulida topilgan qiymat bilan solishtiramiz:

γ=|I1−I1,eg|I1⋅100%=|1,987−1,987|1,987⋅100%=0,00%. \gamma = \dfrac{|I_1 - I_{1,eg}|}{I_1}\cdot 100\% = \dfrac{|1{,}987 - 1{,}987|}{1{,}987}\cdot 100\% = 0{,}00\%.

Ekvivalent generator usuli natijasi kontur toklar va tugun potensiallar usullari natijalari bilan mos keldi.
